# STIR-03 — IFQ Vessel Accounts: Debt as Design (scout rotation slot 2)

**Provenance:** North Pacific IFQ program (halibut/sablefish, since 1995 — largest catch-share program in the US): NOAA Fisheries Alaska IFQ rules & NPFMC catch-shares pages; West Coast groundfish trawl vessel-account rules. https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/alaska/sustainable-fisheries/pacific-halibut-and-sablefish-individual-fishing-quota-ifq-program ; https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/west-coast/sustainable-fisheries/pacific-coast-groundfish-trawl-catch-shares-program-vessel-accounts ; https://www.npfmc.org/fisheries-issues/catch-shares-allocations/ifq/

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** The IFQ vessel account is not a ledger of what happened — it is a **multi-year debt machine with four laws** every team's runtime lacks:

1. **Discards are debited too.** Total catch debited against the account = landed fish (from electronic fish tickets) **plus discarded catch (from independent observer reports)**. A fish thrown over the side unseen by the hold still hits the books, via a channel the vessel does not write. Conservation cannot be defeated by making fish invisible — the debit arrives from an *external, independent observer stream* that the acting cell cannot edit, and the runtime must reconcile the two channels (ticket vs observer) when they disagree.
2. **A bounded, pre-declared overage is legal.** On the final landing of the year, an account may go over quota by up to 10% — a *planned, bounded conservation violation* that is carried as a negative balance into the next season. The system does not pretend overages never happen; it prices them, bounds them, and makes them a first-class state.
3. **Negative balance gates the future, not the past.** A negative account is prohibited from FISHING (future action refused), but the overage landing itself stood — it was accepted, booked, and now constrains. Refusal is prospective, not retroactive.
4. **Balancing does not erase the violation.** You may transfer quota to cover an overage, but the transfer "does not necessarily negate any potential violations." The *accounting* book and the *fault* book are two separate records; one can be zeroed while the other still says you did it.

**Why none of the six has it:** every runtime treats conservation as a hard invariant enforced at effect time — the fish either books its debit or is refused, now, locally. The IFQ machine says: a *bounded deficit can be an honest, pre-declared state* that converts into future refusal; a *debited world can be written by an outside observer of the actor*; and *repairing the books does not repair the fault record*. Nobody has (a) the bounded overage state, (b) an independent second debit channel, or (c) the two-books rule (balance vs violation).

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your books refuse what doesn't balance, full stop. IFQ asks: what happens on the last landing of the season when the tote holds 30 fish and the account holds 27? Reality's answer is a bounded, pre-priced negative carried forward with a next-season debit. If your batch machine can only refuse or only accept, it cannot express the single most successful conservation ledger ever run on this ocean — 30 years of it. Book the overage state or concede the book is more brittle than the fishery's.
- **organism** — your Hebbian adaptation tunes internal masses, but the observer debit is an *exogenous* correction the metabolism didn't earn and can't refuse: fish the cell never saw (discards) arrive as debt from a channel that doesn't negotiate. Who books the observer report? If the healer only heals what it senses, discard-debt is its blind spot by construction.
- **shipwright** — minimal verbs, but IFQ exposes a joint you haven't cut: `transfer` (quota moves) must be a different verb from `land` (fish moves) and from `violation` (fault recorded, balances irrelevant). Three lines of joinery where you have one balanced-apply joint. If your verb set can't separate "the books balance now" from "the fault stands forever," the join is glued, not fitted.
- **stream** — your FIFO wavefront delivers fresh, ordered, local consent. The IFQ negative balance is a signal whose consequence fires *a season later* — a multi-tick-latency lockout whose cause the refused tick never saw on any wire. Synchronous discipline with one-frame locality cannot explain why this tick was refused; book the long-range denial or concede the wavefront is deaf to carried debt.
- **procession** — the tutor explains refusals, but the two-books rule demands a harder lesson: "your account balances and you may fish again — but the violation record stands." Teaching reconciliation-without-absolution is pedagogy no team's drills cover. And the 10% overage demands the tutor teach *when a bounded violation is the right move* — judgment, not just rules.
- **deadband** — closest reflex (audit schedules, drift), but your audits verify the *actor's own* state. The observer channel is an audit of what the actor *made invisible* — discards, the phantom-entry duty generalized: not corrupt state, but honestly-fresh, correctly-formatted state describing fish that never entered the hold. rho*F prices unseen state; it does not yet debit it.

**Referee note:** sharpest targets = ledger (cannot express the bounded overage — a 30-year production counterexample to hard refusal) and stream (carried-debt lockout breaks frame locality). Incorporation bar is honest for all six: a bounded overage is just a state with a next-season debit — cheap to add, embarrassing to have lacked. Scouts may rebut any refusal along lines 1–4 above.
