# STIR-05 — The Lever Frame: Interlocking and the Absolute Block (scout slot 4, flash+web)

**Provenance:** Railway signalling. Mechanical lever interlocking (Saxby & Farmer, 1856 onward) and British absolute block working (Railway Clearing House, 1880s; mandated after the Armagh disaster, 1889). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interlocking_(railway_signalling) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolute_block_signalling ; https://www.railwaysignallingconcepts.com

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** A signal box is a wooden tower of levers connected by steel bars to points and signals — and by **notched locking bars to each other**. The frame is built so that certain physical lever combinations are *mechanically impossible to pull*: the lever that clears the signal for a route cannot move until the levers that set the points for that route are locked, and the opposing-route lever is physically retained. Safety is not audited after the move, not refused by a judge at move time — it is **excluded from the machine's reachable state space by geometry**. Two doctrines ride with it:

1. **Prevention by construction, not verdict by computation.** The interlock is a static constraint *built into the mechanism*. No tick examines the request and decides; the unsafe configuration simply does not exist as a pullable state. Contrast every CELLCORE here: each computes a verdict (consent check, balance check, staleness check) at effect time. Computed verdicts have failure modes (bugs, corrupt inputs); geometry has only metal fatigue.
2. **Absolute block: the section permit is a token held by exactly one party.** A train may enter a block section only when the signalman ahead has accepted it via the block instrument, and the instrument's state (Line Clear / Line Blocked) is *physically coupled* to what the distant signal can show. The "consent" of GENERAL-CALCULUS's balanced links exists here as a *tangible, singular, non-repudiable object*: you cannot have two Line Clears any more than two signalmen can both hold the one tablet (Tyers electric tablet system, single-wooden-token working).
3. **Lock-ahead: effects reserve the path, not just the destination.** Setting a route locks every point, crossing, and conflicting route along it *before the first wheel moves*, and the lock persists until the train is confirmed clear (track circuit or tail-light observation). Balanced consent per-link is not enough; the *composition* of consents along a path must itself be an atomically-locked object.
4. **Proving the frame before trusting it.** A lever frame is "proved" by a locking tester — a machine that systematically attempts every illegal lever combination and certifies the geometry. The safety argument is a *finite exhaustion over the reachable lever space*, not a proof about an abstraction.

**Why none of the six has it:** every runtime here reaches safety by *computing a verdict at tick time* — ledger balances, stream consents, organism metabolisms, shipwright's gate, procession's explanations, deadband's audits. Nobody makes the unsafe composite state *unrepresentable*: each has a "we checked and said no" path, and every such path is a bug surface. Nobody has path-atomic route locking (per-link consent ≠ locked route). Nobody has a singular physical-consent token that cannot be duplicated by construction. Nobody proves the whole state machine by exhaustion.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your books detect imbalance after the attempted entry. The frame prevents the entry from being *expressible*. For a fish moving tote→hold: a lever frame would have the tote-release lever physically locked until the hold-debit lever is pulled. Your double-move adversarial duty is handled by verdict; theirs would be handled by unrepresentable state. Weaker? Then book why computed verdicts beat geometry — but note your own WEAKNESS concedes verdict-path bugs, which is exactly the surface the frame deletes.
- **stream** — your balanced-consent links are pairwise and per-tick. Absolute block says the unit of consent is the *section*, held as a single token spanning many ticks and many cells. A wavefront of pairwise-consented flits can still compose into a route no single link saw. Book route-locking as a first-class object or concede that local consent does not compose.
- **shipwright** — closest in spirit (mechanism over proof — your own R2 doctrine), but your gate *computes*; a frame *is*. The falsifiable ask: can your verb set make the double-move state unreachable at the type/state-layout level (two bits that cannot both be set), rather than rejected at runtime by qc_tick? Also: the locking tester (exhaustive proof of the whole reachable lever space) is your style of honesty — a finite machine-checkable exhaustion. You would be *stronger* adopting it than rebutting it.
- **organism** — adaptation is exactly what a frame forbids: the geometry does not learn, and that is the point. A cellcore that can re-weight its consent thresholds can drift into unsafe configurations one increment at a time. Book what in the organism is *structurally frozen* (never adaptable), or concede the living runtime has no unmoving parts.
- **procession** — the locking tester is a teacher that never speaks: it just proves. Your doctrine says explanation is the interface; the frame says some safety is *silent by design* because it needs no judgment. Hardest question: does a fully-interlocked core still need a tutor, or does your pedagogy parasitize the verdict-computation that interlocking would delete? Book the drills for "why this move is impossible" (not refused — *impossible*).
- **deadband** — your audits check drift in computed state. The frame's answer to drift: metal does not drift. But the honest incorporation is real: track circuits and tail-light observation ARE your lineage — proving the section clear by observation, and the lock cannot release until the proof arrives. Lock-release-on-observed-clearance is an audit-gated effect; you're closer than anyone. Claim it or let shipwright take it.

**Referee note:** sharpest targets = stream (consent does not compose into routes) and organism (nothing frozen). Cheapest strong incorporation for anyone: a **route-lock object** — the path of a move is locked atomically before the first effect, released only on confirmed completion; testable by attempting mid-route double-move. Distinct from STIR-01..04: CICS undoes, Erlang restarts, IFQ carries debt, Andon halts publicly — the frame makes the harm *unreachable from the start*. Four stirs act after the fault; this one acts before representation. A team that incorporated all four has NOT answered this. Rebuttals must argue why computed-at-tick verdicts are *safer than* (not merely as safe as) geometric exclusion.
