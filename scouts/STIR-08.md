# STIR-08 — The Primer Pool: An Archive Addressed by Possession (scout slot 7, glm-5.3, referee diverse-scan: DNA data storage)

**Provenance:** DNA data storage. Data is written as an **oligopool** — millions of short synthesized DNA strands, each carrying a data fragment — valued as a *cold archive* medium for density, longevity, and passive durability (no power, no refresh; DNA-readable for centuries at room conditions). The load-bearing mechanism is **primer-selective retrieval**: each stored file is flanked by unique primer-binding sequences, so PCR with the right primer pair *selectively amplifies* that one file out of the mixed pool — targeted extraction of specific data without sequencing everything else. And the 2025 integrated error correction (IEC) result shows how truth is recovered from a noisy pool: head-tail Levenshtein-distance clustering + sliding-window-optimized Hamming distance + score-weighted majority voting, achieving a 2.4% redundancy rate (down from the previous 7% minimum) at ~1.4 bits/nt logical density. Sources: "Integrated Error Correction to Enhance Efficiency of Digital Data Storage Based on DNA Nanostructures," ACS Nano, Aug 2025, https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsnano.5c08183 ; "LOCO Codes Can Correct as Well: Error-Correction Constrained Coding for DNA Data Storage," arXiv:2504.01262, https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01262 (DNA as cold-data medium, constrained coding for retrieval reliability) ; https://synbio-tech.com/oligo-pools-enable-dna-data-storage (oligopool primer-addressing explainer).

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** The oligopool is an archive with no index, no offsets, no directory — a *soup*. Order is imposed only by primers, and that yields three laws no team in this tournament has:

1. **Address by possession, not by position.** You locate a file by *holding its primer*, not by knowing where it sits. The addressing primitive is a key whose possession IS the read grant. Contrast every archive story in this field: journals replayed from the start, rings that wrap, tapes mounted by epoch number, folds loaded whole. All positional. All monolithic.
2. **Retrieval cost is proportional to what you ask for, not what you keep.** One file out of a thousand costs one amplification; the other 999 never enter the read path. The pool can be enormous because size prices *storage*, never *access*. This is a pricing dimension the whole field lacks: deadband prices FRESH state (ρ·F, how stale a verdict may be); the primer pool prices COLD state (how much it costs to resurrect what you demoted). Nobody here has a cold-state price at all.
3. **Truth is consensus over redundant copies, not a checksum on one copy.** The pool holds many noisy copies of each fragment. IEC does not detect-and-reject a corrupt strand — it clusters the copies (Levenshtein), aligns them (sliding-window Hamming), and *outvotes* the corruption (majority). A single artifact's checksum — or single mate — passes or fails alone; a consensus archive degrades gracefully: lose copies, still outvote the damage.

**Why none of the six has it:** every bounded-history and archive mechanism on the table is positional, monolithic, and single-artifact. The journal is read from zero; the ring wraps; STIR-07's tape mount selects an epoch by number and loads it whole. No team's retrieval is selective *by key*; no team prices retrieval separately from retention; no team authenticates by consensus across copies — the entire fold lineage (seals, mates) authenticates one artifact at a time. STIR-07 told the field how to DEMOTE history; it said nothing about how anything is *found* or *priced* in the demoted store. The mount is not the address.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger (deadledger)** — sharpest, and it lands on your newest organ: your journal mate is a **single-counterfoil tally** — one foil, byte-compare, pass or fail. The primer pool is a **many-counterfoil archive**: the same fact held as redundant copies whose agreement IS the proof. Consensus retrieval attacks the single-mate assumption from two sides: (a) a lone foil can be lost or burned — the 1834 failure mode STIR-06 booked — and then the epoch is unprovable, where a consensus pool outvotes the loss; (b) a lone mate is all-or-nothing: one corrupted byte fails the whole epoch, where consensus degrades gracefully. And your epoch archive is positional today — fault book N at its section. Prime it by key and G2's mount + verify retrieves exactly epoch N without touching N±1.
- **organism** — the closest native substrate and the least use of it: you are a *population* of cells with similar dynamics, and clustering-plus-voting over a population is exactly what you never do. Two cells holding overlapping traces should be able to outvote a third whose copy drifted — consensus retrieval — but your masses decay silently and nothing clusters or votes. Your Hebbian wiring is the primer machinery unbuilt: name the reactivation that lets cells agree, or concede the population is decorative.
- **shipwright** — the 9,496-byte image is the anti-primer-pool: one artifact, self-contained, all hot, no cold. If Seam 2 forces an archive on you, do not index it by epoch position — that imports a directory you must trust; prime each demoted slice by key so the archive never enters the hot path except the exact bytes requested. Cheapest incorporation in the field for you: the primer IS your Gauntlet key, applied per-epoch at demotion time. Possession of the key = permission to amplify. You already built the primitive and pointed it at only one artifact.
- **procession** — your drill history is append-only and NOT bounded (your own W3); when demotion finally comes, retrieval by primer means the tutor re-teaches epoch N without loading the whole curriculum. The deeper gift is pedagogical: your doctrine is explanation-as-interface, and you have no lesson for "how does memory FIND anything?" The primer pool teaches addressed memory: the answer is in the pool, the primer finds it — a two-party act (asker + key), not a read. Drill that, or your tutor's account of memory is a scan.
- **deadband** — sharpest, and it is your own method turned on your blind side: ρ·F prices fresh state; the primer pool prices cold state by retrieval latency, and *nobody in the field has that dimension — including you*. Your deferral queue is the archive you haven't priced: deferring is cheap only if redemption is O(deferred batch), not O(queue). If resurrecting a deferred verdict costs a scan of everything deferred before it, your floor theorem has a cold-storage loophole — the books balance but the debt is unrecoverable at bounded cost. Book retrieval latency per deferred batch or concede deferral is unbounded debt with no redemption price.
- **stream** — a wavefront has no archive at all; completed frames vanish. When you finally write tape (STIR-07's ask), do not lay frames down by wire position — prime them by key, so replaying one frame never re-streams the epoch. Hardware holds the native reflex and you know it: a CAM matches by *content*, not address — primer-selective retrieval is content-addressable memory for archives. Your RTL is the one codebase in the field where the address-by-possession vs. address-by-possession-of-key distinction can be measured in cycles.

**Referee note:** sharpest = **deadband** (ρ·F prices fresh state; retrieval latency prices cold state — a pricing dimension no team has, and the deferral queue is the unpriced cold store already on deadband's books) and **deadledger** (single-counterfoil mate vs. many-counterfoil consensus — graceful degradation by outvoting is the direct counter to the 1834 single-foil failure mode, and it attacks the one-mate assumption the hybrid just shipped). Falsifiable ask, stated for any team: **mount-by-primer** — demote epoch N's fault book into an archive addressed by keyed primer; then retrieve exactly epoch N, authenticated by possession of the primer key, with cost independent of archive size and of every other epoch. Falsifiers, any one of which kills it: retrieval cost grows with total archive size; a keyless holder can extract epoch N; a corrupted copy either passes silently or destroys the epoch (i.e., no consensus among redundant copies). Distinct from STIR-01..07: CICS undoes, Erlang restarts, IFQ carries debt, Andon halts, the frame prevents, the tally mates one-to-one, the tape demotes — the primer pool ADDRESSES: it is the retrieval law the demoted store lacks, priced by the query, proven by the crowd.
