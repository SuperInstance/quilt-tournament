# STIR-09 — Sleep: The Scheduled Replay Phase (scout slot 8, glm-5.3, referee diverse-scan: systems consolidation)

**Provenance:** Mammalian systems consolidation. During NREM sleep, hippocampal **sharp-wave ripples** re-activate traces of waking experience, coordinated with cortical **slow oscillations** and thalamocortical **spindles** into a nested cascade that drives reactivation and gradual reorganization: dependence on the trace *migrates* from hippocampus to neocortex over days to years, and remote memories survive hippocampal damage — the hot store can be lost after the long store has integrated. The load-bearing detail: consolidation is **reactivation-based, not copy-based** — the neocortex does not receive a transcript; it *rebuilds* the memory into existing cortical structure (schema integration). The trace is not moved; it is re-derived and re-woven until the journal can be let go. Sources: "Memory Consolidation" (review; NREM replay, hippocampus-to-neocortex migration, remote-memory survival), PMC4526749, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4526749/ ; https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2123432119 (computational work on hippocampo-cortical consolidation during simulated sleep — replay drives the reorganization) ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_consolidation .

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** The brain schedules its own forgetting, and the schedule is a *phase*, not a policy. Four laws:

1. **There is an offline phase, on a clock.** The system periodically stops taking the world in and re-activates recent traces (replay). Not on-demand, not continuous, not best-effort — a standing appointment. Every tick in this tournament is "awake."
2. **What fails to replay is what fades.** Compaction is selection *by reactivation*, not by age, size, or position: a trace earns retention by being re-activated and integrated; what is never replayed decays. The garbage collector is the scheduler.
3. **The hot store is capacity-bounded BY DESIGN, and its overflow is resolved by integration — never by deletion of what never replayed.** The hippocampus is small on purpose; its bound is not a leak to fix but the forcing function that makes consolidation run. Bounded hot + unbounded long, joined by a transform.
4. **The transform is re-derivation, not transcription.** The long store rebuilds the trace into structure it already owns; afterwards the structure answers what the raw trace answered. Bounded history is achieved by *turning history into structure* — not by discarding it, and not merely by shelving it.

**Why none of the six has it:** every bounded-history mechanism on the table removes, shelves, or wraps ENTRIES. Kafka keeps the last value per key and deletes the rest; LSM merges away old versions; pruning discards blocks; the tape (STIR-07) demotes epochs intact. None of them re-derives an *integrated structure* and retires the source entries because the structure now answers for them. And none has an offline phase: no team schedules time where the system is closed to the world and works only on itself. The field has undo, restart, debt, halt, prevention, mating, demotion — it has no sleep.
