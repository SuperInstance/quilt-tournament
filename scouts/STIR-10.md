# STIR-10 — The Lease: Truth That Must Be Renewed (scout slot 9, flash+web, diverse-scan: authority with expiry)

**Provenance:** Two production systems that solved the same problem from opposite ends. **Google Chubby** (Burrows, OSDI '06): every lock grant carries a **lock generation number** that increments on every free→held transition, and a **sequencer** — an opaque token (lock name, mode, generation) the holder presents to servers to *prove* the grant; a holder presenting an old generation after a re-grant is structurally rejected, so a late or zombie authority can never act even if the network lies about when it died. **DHCP**: an address is not owned, it is **leased** — bound with two staggered timers, T1 (renew, unicast to the grantor) and T2 (rebind, broadcast to *anyone* who can renew), and expiry is a **state transition back to INIT**, not an error, not a panic, not a corrupted system. Source: "The Chubby lock service for loosely-coupled distributed systems," Burrows, OSDI 2006, https://www.usenix.org/legacy/event/osdi06/tech/burrows.html (mirror: https://www.cs.fsu.edu/~awang/courses/cop5611_s2026/chubby.pdf ); DHCP lease FSM with T1/T2 renewal/rebinding, http://www.tcpipguide.com/free/t_DHCPLeaseRenewalandRebindingProcesses-2.htm .

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** In this tournament, a fact once booked stays booked until something *rebuts* it. The lease inverts the default: **nothing stays true without renewal; truth expires by omission, not by fault.** Four laws:

1. **Grants, not facts.** Authority (a consent, a seat, a booked verdict, an adaptation) is issued with a deadline. Holding it past the deadline is not a bug to catch — it is *unrepresentable*: the tick in which the lease lapses demotes the holder automatically, by state transition, with no detector needed.
2. **Expiry is a state, not an error.** DHCP does not raise an exception at lease end; it walks to INIT and re-requests. A lapsed consent in CELLCORE must be a first-class state (like STIR-02's error-as-state) — the link returns to un-consented, and re-consent is an ordinary transaction, not a recovery path.
3. **Renewal is a graded ladder, not a cliff.** T1: renew with the original grantor. T2: renew with *anyone* empowered to renew. Only then lapse. Authority degrades through two fallbacks before it dies — the holder is given every cheap chance to stay legitimate before the expensive one (full re-request) is forced.
4. **Every re-grant bumps the generation.** Chubby's deep trick: even a *timely-looking* old authority is refused, because the generation number is structural. A zombie cell that missed its own lapse cannot act on a consent that has since been re-granted — the stale grant fails byte-comparison against the current generation, no timing argument, no float, no clock skew debate.

**Why none of the six has it:** every authority mechanism on the table is **permanent until revoked** — consents held indefinitely, verdicts booked forever, masses decayed but never *demoted by state*, restart intensity budgets that are reactive meters rather than expiring grants. STIR-02's restart budget reacts after excess; STIR-04's andon halts after a pull; STIR-06 authenticates possession of a *permanent* pair. No team has authority whose *default is expiry* — where the system's resting state is "nobody may act," and acting is something you continuously re-earn. That inverts every trust story in the field: the field asks "what proves you may?" — the lease asks "what proves you may *still*?"

**Threat table:**
- **ledger (deadledger)** — your consents and seats are booked facts, immortal until a balancing entry. The lease asks: what if every authorization in the books carried a generation and a T1/T2 ladder? A double-move attack that relies on an old consent becomes *structurally void* — the phantom-entry duty gets a second, cheaper wall. But it lands on your deepest bet: the ledger's whole ethics is permanence — the book never forgets. Distinguish sharply or be conflated: the *record* of the grant is permanent (book it); the *authority* expires (generation bump). Book the distinction or concede the lease is inexpressible in a ledger of immortal facts.
- **stream** — sharpest, and it is your native hardware: a lease IS a timing property, and you already own timing. A wavefront can implement generation-checked grants in RTL with a counter and a comparator — the zombie grant is refused in one cycle, which no software team can match. But your frame discipline has no *renewal ladder*: a consent that lapses mid-frame either wedges the wavefront or gets silently carried. Wire the T1/T2 ladder (renew from the grantor cell, then from any cell in the domain, then lapse-to-unconsented) as a bounded, cycle-counted state machine — or concede that your synchronous discipline handles death but not decay of authority.
- **organism** — you are the accused again: your Hebbian masses decay, but decay-of-mass is *diffusion*, not *lease-lapse* — it weakens gradually, never demotes structurally, and nothing re-grants. STIR-09 asked why your adaptation isn't sleep; this asks why your plasticity isn't leases. Cell authority that must be re-earned (re-activated) or structurally lapse would finally give your adaptation a *verdict-relevant* job: an un-renewed cell cannot consent, which the conformance pipeline can measure. Decline, and your metabolism remains a mood, not a mechanism.
- **shipwright** — you have the inverted problem again: a fixed image is all-generation-zero, nothing expires because nothing persists dynamically. The lease is your cheapest test of the image's honesty: can your minimal verbs even *express* "this consent lapses at tick N"? If expressing a lease costs you three new verbs, your joinery claim bends; if it costs one counter and one comparison, book that number loudly. Either way the exercise names what your craft trades away for stillness.
- **procession** — pedagogical gold: your tutor drills operators on what to do, but never on *when their own authority ends*. A tutor lineage that teaches an operator "your badge expires; here is the renewal ladder; here is what you may NOT do in the lapse window" is teaching judgment, not procedure — the PLATO doctrine applied to time. And your drill licenses themselves should be leases: a certificate of competence earned once and held forever is a zombie grant in the curriculum. Re-teach or expire.
- **deadband** — the collision is head-on and the referee wants it fought in the open: your ρ·F floor is a *measurement* of staleness — truth degrades continuously with measured staleness. The lease is *structural* staleness — truth lapses discretely by generation, immune to clock-skew arguments. These are rivals, not friends: a freshness floor can be satisfied by a prompt-but-zombie authority; a lease can be current-but-degraded. Book which governs when they disagree — the verdict-schedule meets the grant-schedule — or concede your floor has a zombie loophole: fast answers from dead authority pass ρ·F every time.

**Referee note:** sharpest = **stream** (leases are timing discipline made structural; one-cycle generation rejection is the RTL-native answer no other substrate can give, but the renewal ladder is untested in a wavefront) and **deadband** (ρ·F measurement vs. lease generation is the only head-on doctrine collision in this stir — the freshness floor passes prompt zombies; the lease has no freshness gradient; who wins when they disagree is unbooked). Falsifiable ask, stated for any team: **generation-leased authority** — every consent/grant carries (deadline, generation); a tick past the deadline demotes the holder by state transition (no detector, no error); re-grant bumps the generation; an attempt to act under a lapsed or superseded generation is refused *by generation compare alone*, with no timing or clock argument anywhere in the verdict path; and the T1/T2 ladder is observable as bounded states. Falsifiers, any one of which kills it: acting under a stale generation ever succeeds (even once, even "harmlessly"); expiry raised as an error/exception rather than a state; renewal that cannot proceed when the original grantor is gone (no T2); or a float/clock-skew value anywhere in the lapse decision. Distinct from STIR-01..09: CICS undoes, Erlang restarts, IFQ carries debt, Andon halts, the frame prevents, the tally splits possession, the tape demotes, the primer pool addresses, sleep integrates — the lease **EXPIRES**: authority whose resting state is absence, and whose proof of life is continuously re-earned. A team that adopted all nine prior stirs has NOT answered this. Rebuttals must show why permanent-until-revoked authority is *better than* (not merely simpler than) expiring grants with a renewal ladder.
