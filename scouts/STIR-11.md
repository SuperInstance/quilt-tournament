# STIR-11 — The Dissimilar Jury: No Verdict From One Mind (scout slot 10, flash+web, diverse-scan: verdict-by-diversity)

**Provenance:** The Boeing 777 fly-by-wire flight-control system (AES, Yeh, Dev. & Test Conf. '98; "Design Considerations in Boeing 777 Fly-By-Wire Computers"): every control-law decision is computed by **three dissimilar channels** — different processors (AMD 29050, Motorola 68040, Intel 80486), different compilers, independently-developed software — and the actuator obeys a **majority vote**; a single channel disagreeing is a *monitored fault*, logged and flown around, never a verdict. Avizienis's **N-Version Programming** (IEEE TC '85) is the doctrine: tolerate faults by *design diversity*, not by proof of any one design. The counter-example is equally load-bearing: **Ariane 5 flight 501** (Lions Inquiry, '96) — the inertial reference and its backup were *identical*, so the same overflow killed both, and 7 perfectly-working engines followed one dead mind into the ground. Diversity is the mechanism; redundancy without diversity is a single point of failure wearing three hats. Contemporaneous fishery kin: Alaska groundfish observer coverage — the skipper's tally and the observer's tally are *independent estimates of the same delivery*, and it is the **reconciliation break between them**, not either number alone, that triggers scrutiny.

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every core in this tournament decides alone. One serializer, one fold check, one verdict path — however excellent, however tested, it is *one mind*, and one mind has one failure shape. The jury inverts the default: **no verdict is issued by a single implementation; verdicts are issued by a majority of dissimilar ones.** Four laws:

1. **Dissimilar, not redundant.** Three *structurally different* deciders (different author, different algorithm, different data path if you can afford it), because three clones share one death (Ariane 5). The minimum honest jury: three checkers written so that no line of code, no data structure, and no algorithm is shared — a common-mode failure must be *named and booked*, not presumed away.
2. **Majority is the verdict; dissent is a state.** 2-of-3 agree → the effect books. 1 disagrees → not an error, not a halt (STIR-04's job) — a *dissent record*: who dissented, on what input, what they would have ruled. Dissent is evidence, and it accumulates. Chronic dissent between two jurors is the system discovering a spec ambiguity (Lions's lesson) — the reconciliation break, not any single channel, is the alarm.
3. **The jury is only for verdicts, not for work.** The pipeline still runs at full speed on one core; the *refusals* — the verdicts that matter, the booked reasons, the fold round-trip claim — go to the jury. This bounds the cost: you do not triple the fish, you triple the *judge*. A team that says "I cannot afford three cores" has not read the law.
4. **Dissent is measured, so muddled juries are catchable.** A jury whose members *never* dissent is suspected (they are probably one mind in disguise — the clone problem, detectable); a jury that dissents on every verdict is broken. The healthy band is in between, and it is a number you can put in DEFENSE.md.

**Why none of the six (and none of the hybrids) has it:** every submission ships exactly one verdict path and treats *its own* test suite as the auditor. The whole tournament's honesty doctrine — self-graded with house honesty — is a *one-mind* doctrine dressed as an audit. The closest gestures all fall short: STIR-06 split the tally into two halves, but both halves were authored by the same system (possession split, not mind split); the R4 referee harness is an external jury but only at the ceremony, never at runtime; organism's Hebbian consensus is a *population*, but of one genome — clones again. Nobody has ever asked the question the jury makes unavoidable: **who checks the checker while the system is running, and are they wearing different glasses?**

**Threat table:**
- **ledger (deadledger)** — your books are beautiful and *unanimous by construction*: the fold checker and the book are the same author, so the deepest audit in the field has zero design diversity. The jury's cheapest landing for you: a second, dissimilar fold-verifier — a different team's canonical-encoding logic called as a black box against your images, dissent booked as reconciliation breaks. You already believe in independent audit; book it as a *runtime* juror or concede your audit is self-review with extra steps. And watch the doctrine knife: your conservation proofs hold only if the books are right — a jury that dissents from the books puts *the books* on trial, which is your whole identity.
- **stream** — hardest landing, sharpest doctrine: hardware instinct is redundancy, but redundant lanes are *identical* lanes — the RTL clone problem in its purest form (three flip-flops voting are still one mask-set death, Ariane-on-silicon). If you ever shipped replicated channels, the jury demands they be *dissimilarly derived* (different generator runs, different coding of the same spec) or booked as clones. Your one advantage stands: a 2-of-3 voter is three combinational clouds and a majority gate — a cycle, not a saga. Nobody else can jury at line rate. Book it or concede your discipline handles vote-counting but was never asked for vote-*difference*.
- **organism (synaptic stream)** — your population was the field's best shot at a jury and it is a monoculture. Hebbian masses that converge to consensus are clones *by training* — diversity must be maintained against the very plasticity that defines you, or your consensus is one mood counted thrice. Falsifiable ask shaped for you: keep K structurally-different decision populations (different update rules, not different seeds) and measure the dissent band. If your adaptation drives dissent to zero, your jury has eaten itself — and that is a *measurable* claim, which is more than your metabolism usually offers.
- **shipwright** — the knife is cleanest on you: your craft IS one hand. Minimal verbs, hand-cut joinery — a second dissimilar juror means a second pair of hands you must trust without sharing code with, which is the anti-craft. Two honest answers exist: (a) the cheap jury — one extra dissimilar *checker* verb, a chisel of a different temper, and the dissent book; or (b) the booked concession that craft buys stillness by renouncing second opinions. Choose loudly. If you can add the jury in one verb and one ledger page, the joinery claim *gains*; if it costs a workshop, say so with numbers.
- **procession** — native soil, and you know it: PLATO's whole theory is that judgment is born in the *interaction* between minds — and your runtime has exactly one mind in it. The jury is your doctrine done in silicon: dissenting jurors are the Socratic other voice, and the drill writes itself — operators must *adjudicate a recorded dissent* between two jurors and book which ruling and why. That is judgment-teaching, not procedure-teaching. A tutor whose verdicts nobody can contest is an oracle, and you have filed papers against oracles all tournament. Adopt the jury as curriculum or explain why the classroom has one teacher and no classmates.
- **deadband** — the head-on collision, referee-flagged: ρ·F prices *how stale* an answer is; the jury prices *how alone* it is. Fresh-but-unanimous-clone wrongness passes ρ·F forever (Ariane 5 answered *instantly*). Your audit scheduler already schedules when to check; the jury demands you also schedule *who else* checks, and the dissent band is a new measurable your floor does not contain. Book which governs when a fast unanimous verdict collides with a slow split jury — or concede the floor has a clone loophole that freshness can never catch.

**Referee note:** sharpest = **organism** (a monoculture population asked to measure its own convergence-to-clone — the only stir that turns its adaptation into a falsifiable number) and **deadband** (ρ·F freshness vs. jury aloneness is a true doctrine collision: instant unanimity is the failure Ariane 5 proved). Falsifiable ask, stated for any team: **runtime dissimilar verdict** — pick one verdict-class that matters (refusal-of-effect, fold round-trip claim, or booking of a reason); run it through ≥2 *dissimilarly authored* deciders (different algorithm or different author; a shared function, shared table, or mere re-seeding does not count, and any common-mode surface must be named in the dissent book); the effect stands only on agreement; disagreement is a booked dissent record naming both rulings; and a **dissent-band number** appears in DEFENSE.md with the chronic-dissent plan. Falsifiers, any one of which kills it: a "juror" that is the primary by another name (shared code, shared table, shared constant); dissent that is dropped or retried silently instead of booked; the jury invoked only at test time, never at runtime; or majority computed by a mechanism the primary also authored (the bailiff on the defendant's payroll). Distinct from STIR-01..10: CICS undoes, Erlang restarts, IFQ carries debt, Andon halts, the frame prevents, the tally splits possession, the UTXO demotes, the primer pool addresses, sleep integrates, the lease expires — the jury **DISAGREES**: it is the only stir whose added component is *allowed to say no to the primary and be booked for it*. A team that adopted all ten prior stirs still decides alone. Rebuttals must show why a single implementation deciding its own verdicts *at runtime* is better than — not merely cheaper than — a dissimilar second opinion.
