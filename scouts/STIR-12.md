# STIR-12 — The Watchman's Clock: Silence Is a Hole (scout slot 11, flash+web, presence-scan: nothing-is-a-record)

**Provenance:** The mechanical watchman's tour clock (Detex Guard Tour, early 1900s): the night watchman carries a clock on a chain and must punch *station keys* mounted at fixed points on his rounds. The punch record does not prove nothing happened overnight — it proves **the watchman existed during the hours nothing happened**. Skip a station and the paper tape shows a hole where time passed un-witnessed. Same doctrine, older: lighthouse keepers' logs, where "nothing to report" is a *required daily entry* — the keeper who writes nothing is the emergency, not the sea. Railroad kin: the **"OS" report** — trains On Schedule must be reported at stations even when exactly on time, so dispatchers can tell *on time* from *vanished*. Fishery kin, live law: ADF&G commercial fishery logbooks require an entry **even on zero-catch days** — a day with no row is a violation, not a slow day. The uniform insight across a century of custody systems: **a log of events is only half a record; the other half is the log of intervals where no event occurred.** Without it, "quiet" and "erased" are the same bytes.

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every core in this tournament books *events* — moves, refusals, reasons. None books **time itself**. The clock inverts the default: **every tick produces exactly one record, and a tick in which nothing happened produces a NULL record — a punched nothing.** Three laws:

1. **Presence is a record.** Each local tick appends one entry to the log: an effect, a refusal-with-reason, or an explicit NULL ("ticked; no move; nothing to report"). The log is *time-complete*: its length is the tick count, its gaps are load-bearing structure. Bounded state is preserved because the null is a fixed-size row, not a narrative.
2. **Silence is a hole, and holes are refusals waiting to book.** A fold over a time-complete log can detect what no event-log can: **the compaction attack** — an adversary who deletes a tick's effects and re-seals the log leaves a state that is perfectly consistent and one tick short. Without punched nothings, "the deck was idle" and "the log was cut" are indistinguishable. With them, truncation of *time* is detectable, not just truncation of *data* — the truncated-fold adversarial duty gains a temporal half it currently does not have.
3. **The null must be earned, not assumed.** A gap inferred by timestamps is not a punch — timestamps are claims by the same clock under attack. The punch is a *structural* row whose absence the fold itself flags. Canonical encoding must round-trip nulls as first-class symbols: decode(encode(x)) == x must include the *nothings*, or your fold round-trip claim silently excludes the majority of your runtime's life.

**Why none of the six has it:** every team's log is event-driven — an idle pipeline writes nothing, which means the honest case (quiet) and the attacked case (erased) produce **identical artifacts**. The ledger books moves; the stream carries symbols only when data flows (a FIFO with no idle token has no presence channel); the organism's silence could be sleep or death — metabolically indistinguishable from outside; shipwright's minimal verbs would never spend a line on nothing; procession teaches what happened, never interrogates the gaps; deadband schedules *when to check* but never books *when nothing needed checking* — ρ·F can price a stale answer, but cannot see a deleted question.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your books are event-ledgers; a week of no rows is indistinguishable from a week of erasure. Cheapest landing in the field: a null row per tick (or per batch window with a booked tick count) — you already have the row machinery; this is one more row kind and a fold clause. Refuse it and your conservation proofs hold only over the entries that survived.
- **stream** — native soil, one symbol away: emit an explicit **idle token** every tick so the wavefront's presence channel exists even when the data channel is empty. A synchronous discipline that claims timing correctness but has no per-tick symbol cannot prove a tick happened. You can punch at line rate; nobody else can. Book it or concede your clocks verify data, not time.
- **organism** — a metabolism that can idle silently cannot be told from one that has *died* between observations. The punch is a heartbeat: fixed-cost, fixed-size, mandatory. And it makes your Hebbian drift observable during "quiet" periods — adaptation that happens when nothing happens is currently your least-witnessed process. A null record with a drift checksum is a falsifiable metabolic signature. This is the stir that turns "it's alive" into "here is its pulse."
- **shipwright** — the cost knife cuts cleanest on you: a record of nothing is the anti-minimal verb. Two honest answers: (a) the null is one row and one fold clause — joinery so tight the *absence* of a peg is itself visible in the frame; or (b) the booked concession that craft renounces presence-witnessing. But note the craft argument *for*: a punched clock is exactly how a wooden boat's watch schedule was kept. Choose loudly, with the row count.
- **procession** — pedagogical gold: the drill writes itself — *"the log has a hole at tick 417; explain what could have happened, and book three candidate worlds."* Absence-interrogation is the oldest tutor's move (Socrates taught from what the student *didn't* say). A tutor that only explains events trains operators to trust quiet. Adopt it as curriculum or explain why your classroom never asks about silence.
- **deadband** — the collision is a hug: ρ·F prices stale answers, the punch prices absent questions; together they close the loop — the audit scheduler books when it checked, the null log books when there was nothing to check, and *both* omissions become visible. But the knife exists: your audit schedule is itself an event-log — was an audit skipped or deleted? Only a punched audit-clock answers. Absorb and you gain the temporal dual of your own floor; refuse and the floor has a blind spot shaped exactly like deleted audits.

**Referee note:** sharpest = **organism** (the pulse turns "alive" into a falsifiable per-tick measurable — its least falsifiable claim becomes its most) and **deadband** (absorbs as its own temporal dual — the one stir that completes rather than collides, which the rubric should note is *not* automatically a win: easy absorption can mean the idea was already latent). Falsifiable ask, stated for any team: **time-complete log** — every tick appends exactly one of {effect, refusal, explicit NULL}; NULLs are canonical symbols that survive the fold round-trip; the fold flags any tick-gap as a refusal-class fault; and a **compaction test** appears in the suite: delete one tick's rows from a valid log and show the fold rejects it — without NULLs this test is unpassable, which is the whole point. Falsifiers, any one of which kills it: nulls inferred from timestamps rather than present as structural rows; a log whose length is not the tick count; NULLs that the canonical encoding collapses or elides ("we don't serialize the boring ones" = compaction attack, pre-installed by the author); or presence recorded only at test time. Distinct from STIR-01..11: CICS undoes, Erlang restarts, IFQ carries debt, Andon halts, the frame prevents, the tally splits possession, the UTXO demotes, the primer pool addresses, sleep integrates, the lease expires, the jury disagrees — the watchman's clock **ATTESTS TO NOTHING**: it is the only stir whose added component fires precisely when nothing happened, and whose removal by an attacker is the attack. A team that adopted all eleven prior stirs still cannot tell an idle runtime from an edited one. Rebuttals must show why event-only logging is *safer* than time-complete logging — not merely shorter.
