# STIR-13 — The Two-Man Rule: High-Value Effects Need Two Consents (scout slot 12, glm-turbo, diverse-scan: custody-by-plurality)

**Provenance:** Nuclear weapons have carried **Permissive Action Links** since the 1960s, and the doctrine above the electronics is older: no one person, alone, can authorize a launch — two officers, two keys turned apart beyond one arm's reach, both logged. The same geometry runs through every custody system humans trust with irreversible value: bank vaults under **dual control** (one person holds the combination, a second holds the key, and opening requires both, both signing the vault register); Venetian/Japanese storehouse seals that needed two chops; and — nearest to this tournament's water — the **west coast cannery scale-house ticket**, signed by both the tenderman delivering fish and the buyer's scaler before a single pound crosses into the cannery's books, precisely because fish crossing a scale is an *irreversible transfer of money*. ADF&G fish tickets to this day carry two signatures: the fisher and the buyer's representative. One signature on a fish ticket is not a weak fish ticket; it is an *invalid* one. The uniform insight: **for a small class of high-stakes, irreversible effects, single consent is structurally insufficient — not because the consenter might err, but because the system cannot distinguish a decision from a capture of the decider.**

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every core in this tournament authorizes effects by **local consent of one cell** — the move's source ticks yes and the fish moves. The two-man rule splits authorization of designated high-value effects into **ARM and FIRE, held by different principals**, with three laws:

1. **A small, named high-value class.** Not every effect — the tote-to-hold routine shunt stays single-consent or the pipeline dies of ceremony. The core declares a bounded list of *heavy* effects (species-level deliverables: king and coho half-totes; a hold-seal; a ledger's period-close; anything whose reversal costs more than its execution). Cheap effects keep one consent; the classification itself is booked state, not convention.
2. **Arm ≠ fire, and the arms must be different.** A heavy effect needs an ARM consent from one principal and a FIRE consent from a second; a principal may not arm-and-fire alone — an armed effect that meets its own armer's fire consent is *refused*, booked, as a self-dual fault. Each consent is a separate, canonical, fold-visible record. Between arm and fire the effect exists as bounded *pending* state with a mandatory expiry (this composes with STIR-10, not replaces it: the lease prices the wait, the second key prices the act).
3. **Refusal of either key is itself a booked effect.** A fire-consent that declines must be recorded with a reason — a declined arm is evidence, not silence. And the pairing rule is structural: the fold can verify that no heavy effect in the log ever fired with fewer than two distinct principal signatures, without inspecting anything but the log itself.

**Why none of the six has it:** every team's consent is bilateral *between cells* (source and sink), but unilateral *among principals* — one cell's yes is the whole yes. The ledger books who moved what but never demanded a second signature on the big rows; the stream's synchronous discipline verifies timing of consent, never plurality of it; the organism metabolizes on single signals — one excitation fires adaptation; shipwright's minimal verbs are single-grip by creed; procession explains decisions it never co-signs; deadband audits whether an answer was fresh, never whether the actor was alone.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your lineage is the scale-house; the fish ticket's second signature is *your own heritage* and your books lack it. Cheapest landing: heavy rows carry a two-column signature field; the fold's conservation check gains one clause — heavy rows with a duplicated or single principal fail the fold. Refuse, and your audit-first philosophy books one-signature kings, which is exactly what a cannery refused to do for a century.
- **stream** — hardware-first is native soil: arm/fire is two strobe phases, two separate handshakes, and the self-dual fault is a combinational check no testbench forgives. A pipeline that can carry an armed-pending token with clean timing is a *better* synchronous design, not a burdened one. Or book the concession that the wavefront's consent is single-threaded by physics — but note real rails solved this with dual-relay interlocking; you stand next to STIR-05's lever frame, which already proved your medium does plurality.
- **organism** — the knife: a metabolism where one excitation fires heavy action is a reflex arc, and reflex arcs are *what parasites exploit*. Two-key is not bureaucracy to a living system; it is the ganglion pattern — heavy motion routes through a second node. Your Hebbian machinery can even *learn* the classification of "heavy" (with the bounded list still enforced structurally). Or concede that the organism has no gate any single tick cannot open, and say what that means under adversary.
- **shipwright** — the deep collision, and you know it: minimal verbs say one grip, the two-key says two. The craft-honest answers: (a) arm/fire is *two pegs, one joint* — shaped so the second peg cannot enter until the first is seated, which is joinery, not glue; or (b) the booked concession that craft renounces dual custody below a stated value line, with the line named. Wooden boats kept the two-watch rule at the wheel; your own tradition argues against you. Cut loudly, with the verb count.
- **procession** — the pedagogy writes itself and cuts twice: the runtime's drills can *teach* dual custody (make the operator be the second key and grade their refusals), and the tutor can interrogate single-keyed heavy rows in the log — *"who else should have signed, and where is their reason?"* A tutor that cannot ask "who was alone?" trains operators to trust solo custody. Adopt as curriculum or book why your classroom never asks about the missing witness.
- **deadband** — near-collision, hug-with-knife: ρ·F prices stale consent; the two-key prices *lonely* consent. Staleness and loneliness are orthogonal failure modes — a fresh answer from a captured actor passes every freshness floor you have. Absorb and your audits gain a second question (was the actor alone?); refuse and your floor certifies fresh signatures from the same hand that forged them. But the rubric should watch the easy-absorption trap from STIR-12's note: if the second key slots in without touching your scheduler, it may not have actually landed.

**Referee note:** sharpest = **ledger** (the mechanism is literally the fish ticket — refusing your own lineage's core practice is a defense problem, not an engineering one) and **organism** (single-excitation heavy action is its most attackable structural claim, and the two-key gives it a falsifiable gate where it currently has a vibe). Falsifiable ask, stated for any team: **dual-custody heavies** — a booked high-value class; arm+fire consents from distinct principals as separate fold-visible records; self-dual (same-principal arm-then-fire) refused and booked; declined keys booked with reasons; expiry on armed-pendings; and a suite test where an adversary submits a fully valid heavy effect carrying only one principal's consents and the core must refuse it *while the fish stays visible in the tote* — fail-static totality means the refusal is a state, not an exception. Falsifiers, any one of which kills it: a "high-value class" that is convention or comment rather than booked state; arm and fire recorded as one row (the pairing cannot be checked from the log alone); principal identity floating in (a float or unbounded string deciding custody — banned by house law); an armed-pending that can expire into *fire* rather than refuse; or dual custody applied to everything (ceremony inflation — the classification exists so the pipeline breathes, and a team that two-keys every humpy has dodged the design question, not answered it). Distinct from STIR-01..12: CICS undoes, Erlang restarts, IFQ carries debt, Andon halts, the frame prevents, the tally splits possession (a *record* split for verification), the UTXO demotes state, the primer pool archives, sleep integrates, the lease expires, the jury diversifies *verdicts*, the watchman attests to nothing — the two-man rule gates **AUTHORIZATION ITSELF BY PLURALITY OF ACTORS**: it is the only stir whose added component fires *before* the effect, on the question "who else?", a question no amount of after-the-fact evidence (all twelve prior stirs) can retroactively answer. A team that adopted every prior stir can still perfectly audit, replay, and attest to a king salmon moved by one captured hand. Rebuttals must show why single-principal consent on irreversible heavy effects is *safer* than dual custody — not merely cheaper, and not by quietly redefining "heavy" until the class is empty.
