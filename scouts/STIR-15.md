# STIR-15 — Damage Control: Deliberate Wounding to Save the Hull (scout slot 14, flash+web)

**Provenance:** US Navy damage control doctrine and its two canonical case studies. USS **Forrestal** (1967): flight-deck fire, cascading cook-offs, 134 dead — the failure mode (ordnance cooking off near burning fuel, no pre-set boundaries). USS **Samuel B. Roberts** FFG-58 (1988, Capt. Paul Rinn): Iranian mine strike, 15–21 ft hole, broken keel, ~2,000 tons of water in two main spaces — ship SAVED because watertight boundaries were set *before* the explosion (Rinn ordered general quarters and boundaries on sighting the mines, pre-hit), and the crew fought progressive flooding for hours. Sources: Wikipedia "USS Samuel B. Roberts (FFG-58)"; navy.mil and NPS accounts of Capt. Rinn's lectures; NSTM Chapter 079 (Damage Control) doctrine of **compartmentation, flooding boundaries, and counterflooding**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USS_Samuel_B._Roberts_(FFG-58) ; https://nps.edu/-/capt-paul-rinn-shares-the-story-of-saving-the-uss-samuel-roberts

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Damage control is a doctrine for a runtime that has ALREADY been hit. Five laws no CELLCORE here has:

1. **Set the boundaries before the wound.** Rinn set watertight integrity on *suspicion* (mines sighted), not on evidence (explosion). Containment is a pre-paid, standing state — boundaries installed during calm as a first-class mode of the system, not a reaction computed at damage time. Every team's containment is *computed after the anomaly is detected*; the Navy's is *armed before the anomaly exists*.
2. **The damage board is outside the wounded plant.** DC Central maintains a manual status board (what's flooded, what's on fire, what's isolated) that must keep working when the very compartments it describes are destroyed and the ship's power is down. The map of the damage lives OUTSIDE the damaged territory, updated by runners, and it is allowed to be *stale and approximate* — a partial, hand-kept truth that survives total loss of the system it watches. Contrast: every runtime here keeps its fault book inside its own state, on its own medium, in its own language.
3. **Counterflooding: the healing move is a deliberate, bounded wound.** When one side floods and the ship lists toward capsize, you FLOOD THE OTHER SIDE — intentionally — trading freeboard for level. This is not reversal (STIR-01), not compensation (a new balancing transaction), not debt (STIR-03): it is **adding harm to cancel geometry**, a move that is insane per-move and mandatory per-whole. The verdict "this effect is bad for this cell but the system must book it anyway to survive" is a judgment class no fail-static core can currently express — refusals are per-move, but capsize is a property of the WHOLE state's shape.
4. **Triage is a total order, declared in advance.** Fire before flood, ordnance before both, personnel above all: NSTM pre-ranks casualties so that under chaos, the runtime doesn't reason about priority — it executes a published order. When two faults are concurrent and treatment conflicts, the conflict is resolved by doctrine written in peacetime, not by judgment under fire.
5. **The measure is the ship floats, not the compartments win.** FFG-58's engine room was a total loss, the keel was broken — SUCCESS. Every compartment's local failure was accepted; the system-level invariant (buoyancy, list within bounds) held. No team has an invariant of the whole that is allowed to be satisfied while every sub-invariant is in a booked failed state.

**Why none of the six has it:** every doctrine here is *containment without loss*: refuse the bad move, keep the cell clean, book the reason. Damage control assumes the hit lands, the state is already dirty, and the runtime's job is now to **lose the right things in the right order** while a separate, cruder, outboard map keeps the truth. Nobody has (a) pre-armed containment modes, (b) an outboard damage board that survives the core, (c) the deliberate-wound move, (d) a pre-declared casualty total order, or (e) a whole-ship invariant licensed to hold while parts are in ruins.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your books balance or the entry is refused; counterflooding is an entry that UNBALANCES a compartment deliberately to balance the ship. If your balance equation is per-cell, you cannot express the move that saves the hull. And your journal lives in your state: where is the runner's hand-copy of the damage board that survives the journal's own destruction? Falsifiable ask: burn the journal mid-fold and keep issuing verdicts from an outboard, coarser, hand-kept board.
- **shipwright** — closest in spirit (craft, triage, minimal means) but your verbs are all *honest*: no verb wounds. Cut the counterflood verb or book why joinery forbids it. And the damage board is joinery's deepest bet: the map must be a SEPARATE PIECE, not a face of the same wood — your one-translation-unit unity is the exact architecture the mine refutes.
- **organism** — metabolism heals toward homeostasis; counterflooding heals THROUGH deliberate injury. A Hebbian healer that only ever moves masses toward health cannot produce the survival move. Your closest reflex is the scar — but a scar is a byproduct; a counterflood is a *chosen* wound with a booked reason naming the whole-state invariant it serves.
- **stream** — the wavefront is healthy or it stalls; FFG-58 ran for hours in a dirty, half-flooded, still-fighting state. Your discipline has no mode for "progressively damaged but operational": either the frame is clean or the link refuses. Book a degraded-mode wavefront (some lanes dead, tick continues on the rest, boundary set around the dead lanes BEFORE corruption crosses them) or concede the wavefront is all-or-nothing.
- **procession** — the tutor's finest hour is exactly the moment Rinn trained for: drills BEFORE the mine, so the response under fire is doctrine, not deliberation. Your C2 differential measures tutor vs no-tutor on refusal paths; damage control demands drills measured on *performance under injected degradation* — pre-trained operator vs cold operator when the core is already wounded. That is PLATO's original claim; it is yours to take.
- **deadband** — pre-setting boundaries on suspicion is your lineage's instinct (audit schedules, prefetch before drift). Sharpest ask: your ρ·F prices what you can't see; the mine strike prices what you can't PREVENT. Book the pre-arm cost (boundaries set on suspicion = paid containment capacity held idle during calm) and its refund condition, or concede the runtime only ever audits the war it's already in.

**Referee note:** sharpest targets = stream (no degraded mode — all-or-nothing wavefront is a construction-level gap the mine disproves) and ledger (no counterflood verb; per-cell balance cannot express whole-ship salvation).
