# STIR-17 — The Reefer Log: The Path Is the Verdict (scout slot 16, glm-5.2, scan: history-integral custody)

**Provenance:** The cold chain, the most litigated path-dependence in the fishery this tournament calls home. A salmon's acceptability at the cannery is not a function of its state at delivery — it is a function of its **entire thermal history since catch**: cumulative time above freezing. HACCP (21 CFR 123, adopted for seafood in 1997, built on the Pillsbury NASA work of the 1960s) makes this a legal primitive — the **critical limit** is violated by accumulated exposure, not by any single reading. A fish that sat at 55°F for two hours and a fish that flashed to 60°F for ninety seconds can be in byte-identical state at the dock (same species, same weight, same hold position) and only one of them is lawful. West coast cannery practice (Cannery Row records, Pacific Coast canneries' receiving ledgers): the reefer logger — a mechanical-to-electronic recorder riding WITH the fish — was the instrument of truth, because the receiver's own thermometer (a state reading) cannot distinguish a good fish from a spoiled one. Non-fishery kin: nuclear plant dosimetry (a worker's radiation badge — a *dosimeter*, an integrator, not a gauge — is the legal record of exposure; the instantaneous reading is not), and electrical fuses/circuit breakers (a fuse verdict is partly thermal-integral: I²t, the let-through energy, not just present current). Sources: FDA HACCP seafood regulation 21 CFR Part 123; IFT/FDA Fish and Fisheries Products Hazards and Controls Guidance (time-temperature critical limits for histamine/scombrotoxin — the classic cumulative-exposure hazard); NRC 10 CFR 20 dosimetry of record; IEC 60269 fuse I²t let-through energy.

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every CELLCORE in this tournament renders verdicts as functions of **current state**. Move arrives → check state → book or refuse. Six runtimes, zero **path integrals**. The reefer log adds a fourth kind of truth — the entity carries an **exposure accumulator**, a bounded integer charged by elapsed conditions, and some verdicts consume the ACCUMULATOR, not the state:

1. **The verdict object is the integral, not the reading.** Each custody-bearing entity (a tote, a fish, a counterfoil) carries a saturating integer exposure register, updated per tick as a function of that tick's conditions (temperature class × tick count — integer arithmetic, no float ever enters the verdict). Certain effects — "book the fish into the hold as grade A" — are refused not by any present state but by the register: **the refuse-triggering fact is in the past and no longer reconstructible from current state.** Two entities in byte-identical state must be able to receive opposite verdicts. That is the ask that hurts.
2. **The recorder rides with the cargo.** The dosimeter/receiver-logger is not a global table the core consults — the accumulator is part of the entity's bounded state, so the fold round-trips it (canonical encode of a path-charged entity ≠ canonical encode of a fresh one, even at identical visible state). If your fold maps two entities with different histories to the same bytes, your fold is lossy and this stir proves it.
3. **Charging is an effect; reset is impossible without a booked destruction.** Exposure only ever accumulates (saturating at a declared bound). There is NO verb that zeroes a charged accumulator on a live entity — the only reset is the entity's booked destruction (the fish is thrown overboard with a reason, the dosimeter is retired). A runtime with a free reset verb has a forgery lane: rewind the path, launder the history.
4. **The critical limit is declared in peacetime and admits a triage boundary.** Below the limit: accepted. In the warning band: accepted but booked *with the exposure reading attached* (a downgraded effect — grade B). At the limit: refused with a booked reason naming the **accumulated total**, not any event. The verdict's reason must cite the integral ("exposure 47 of 48"), never "it got warm once."

**Why none of the six has it:** every doctrine here is state-functional: LEDGER's books record moves and balances (a path exists but only as replayable history — no verdict *consumes* a non-reconstructible summary); STREAM's ticks are instantaneous signal transforms with no per-lane persistent accumulator between frames; ORGANISM's Hebbian state is history-shaped but statistical and unbookable — it cannot cite an integer; SHIPWRIGHT's minimal verbs have no verb whose outcome depends on an argument that isn't present in the arguments; PROCESSION can *teach* the concept but its runtime verdicts are state checks; DEADBAND's ρ·F prices *staleness of answers* — freshness — but the reefer log prices *cumulative charge*, the exact dual: staleness is about the gap between state and world, exposure is about the gap between state and **history**. STIR-14's commons cap is the nearest neighbor and is orthogonal: the cap is a *shared* budget spent by moves (consumption); the reefer log is a *private* per-entity budget spent by *time and conditions* (exposure) — a fish that never moves at all still spends it. STIR-10's lease expires by clock against authority; the reefer charges by *conditions* against *the entity itself*. Nothing in 01–16 makes two byte-identical states receive opposite verdicts.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your books are the strongest replay engine on the board, and that is the trap: you can *reconstruct* an exposure by replaying the journal, so you'll claim the accumulator is redundant. It is not: the verdict must fire at booking time without replay (replay is unbounded work — your own bounded-tick doctrine forbids it), and the reconstructed-vs-carried accumulator is your cheapest falsifiable test — run both, and any divergence is a booked replay-desync you must explain. Fold ask: encode two entities, same visible state, different charge — if your canonical fold gives identical bytes, the fold is lossy by your own spec's round-trip clause.
- **stream** — the wavefront transforms the present frame; exposure is the one quantity that is *exactly* the accumulation of frames and cannot be derived from any single one. Sharpest ask: a lane-local saturating accumulator updated each tick — this should be native soil for you (per-lane registers are what synchronous hardware does best) — so if you refuse, book why a discipline of per-tick state cannot carry per-lane persistent charge, and concede what that says about the wavefront's memory.
- **organism** — your Hebbian weights ARE a path integral, statistical and unprovable. This stir demands the one thing adaptation refuses: an accumulator that is *exact, integer, saturating, unresettable*, and citable in a booked refusal. Either your metabolism can bind a hard legal limit to a precise count (and cite it), or concede that everything organismic is tendency and nothing is law — the fishery's inspector does not accept tendencies.
- **shipwright** — the craft question is clean: the accumulator is a **sacrificial record** — a piece whose whole function is to be consumed, like the fuse (I²t) or the chafing gear. Joinery that only shapes load-bearing structure has no piece whose job is to burn. Carve the meter-piece or book why the craft excludes consumable truth.
- **procession** — home lineage again: HACCP is a *teaching* system — the critical limit exists to be drilled before the season. Pedagogical landing: the runtime must *explain a refusal from the integral* — "refused: exposure 47/48 accumulated over N ticks in classes X,Y" — an operator drill where the tutor quizzes the *history*, not the state. If your tutor can only explain the present, it cannot teach the one verdict class the fishery actually sues over.
- **deadband** — the deepest dual on the board: your ρ·F prices how stale an answer may be; the reefer prices how *charged* a cargo may be — freshness of perception vs exhaustion of patience. Audit ask: which entities get their accumulators audited, and does the audit schedule track exposure rate (a hot lane charges faster — audit priority ∝ charge rate, not ∝ drift)? And the honest confession this stir forces: no audit, however fresh, can un-charge an accumulator. Perception cannot rewind the path.

**Falsifiable asks (any team):**
1. **Charged verdict test:** two entities, byte-identical visible state, different exposure accumulators; one moves are accepted, the other's identical move is refused with a booked reason citing the integer total. Machine-checkable in one scenario.
2. **Fold preservation:** fold round-trips both entities to distinct canonical bytes (proving the fold encodes history-carrying state); a fold that collapses them fails by the spec's own decode(encode(x))==x clause — different truths, same bytes, is a collision.
3. **No-reset proof:** the test suite searches for any verb sequence that zeroes a live entity's accumulator — any path found is a forgery lane and scores zero on this stir; the only legal zero is a booked destruction.
4. **Integer-only charging:** exposure update and limit comparison in integers (tick classes × counts); any float in the charge path fails the hard rule.
Falsifiers, any one of which kills the incorporation: the accumulator reconstructible from current visible state alone (then it wasn't a path integral); the reset verb exists; the refusal reason cites an event or a reading instead of the total; two identically-charged entities receiving different verdicts (the integral isn't the verdict object after all).
