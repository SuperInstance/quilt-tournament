# STIR-19 — The Observer Aboard: The Independent Tally That Owns Nothing (scout slot 18, flash+web, Champion's Gauntlet round)

**Provenance:** The **North Pacific Observer Program** (NMFS / AK region; also ADF&G dockside samplers): every federally observed vessel must carry a *disinterested third party* who counts catch, samples species composition, and monitors compliance — **independent of the crew's own logbook**. The observer does not skipper, does not haul, does not own the fish, and has no stake in the tally. But when the observer's count and the vessel's self-reported logbook diverge beyond tolerance, **the delivery is held** — not because either number is wrong, but because *two independently-derived numbers about the same events disagree*. Kin systems: election observer missions (OSCE/UN — the count is legitimate when independent watchers replicate it), double-entry's original form (the merchant's book vs. the counterparty's book — the cross-check IS the audit), bank reconciliation statements, and the physics of **independent measurement**: no single instrument vouches for itself. Sources: NMFS North Pacific Observer Program (50 CFR 679), ADF&G dockside sampling programs, OSCE election observation methodology (parallel vote tabulation).

## The rival mechanism (distilled)

Every core in this tournament has exactly **one narrative of what happened**: its own state, its own fold, its own booked reasons. Even LEDGER — the book IS the program — has one book. STIR-11 made verdicts disagree transiently; STIR-12 made attestation attest to nothing. Nobody has the observer: **a second, structurally independent derivation of the same history, whose only job is to disagree out loud.** Three laws:

1. **Dual derivation of every countable fact.** Every quantity the core renders a verdict on (totes moved, hooks cleared, debits booked, fish in hold) is computed twice by *non-communicating* mechanisms that share only the raw event stream — never intermediate state, never each other's totals. The observer lane must be architecturally incapable of reading the core's counters: it rebuilds its tally from the same booked events by a different route (different ordering, different accumulator shape, even a different arithmetic path — saturating vs. wrapping, per-cell vs. global-first).
2. **Divergence is a hold, not an error.** When the two tallies disagree at reconciliation, the affected transfer is **held pending re-derivation** — refused forward progress with a booked reason that cites *the gap itself* (both numbers, both routes), never an accusation about which one is right. The core does not adjudicate its own divergence; it makes divergence impossible to spend through. Like the delivery held at the dock: the fish sit until the tally reconciles or the discrepancy is booked as a permanent record with both counts preserved.
3. **The observer lane pays no verdicts and holds no custody.** It cannot refuse a move, cannot book a debit, cannot be a consent party (contra STIR-13 — it never consents, only counts). Its output is exactly one thing: a number, plus the route used to derive it. A core where the observer lane can *cause* any effect has failed the stir — independence is structural, not behavioral.

## Booked challenge to every team

> **Your core vouches for itself on every verdict. Where is the second, independent count of the same events, and what happens at the tick where it disagrees with yours? If your answer is "my fold/round-trip proves my count," remember: a round-trip proves *consistency with your own encoding*, not *agreement with events*. One book is one witness. Show the second — or book why one witness suffices.**

- **LEDGER**: your deepest bet is the book's self-consistency. The observer says: a perfectly balanced book can still be perfectly wrong about the deck. Cheapest landing for you — a parallel journal derived from raw effects, reconciled per batch. But your fold-canonical doctrine now faces two canonical encodings of the same history that must *differ in shape and agree in sum*.
- **STREAM**: you already have spatial structure — but every lane ticks the same synchronous discipline, so a shared systematic error (off-by-one in the frame boundary) corrupts all lanes *identically*. Independent derivation means an observer lane with a **different tick phase** counting the same transactions. Can your wavefront tolerate a counter that lags half a frame and must still land on the same totals?
- **ORGANISM**: adaptation is a *distorting lens by design* — tendencies shape state, so your tally of your own state is shaped by your history. The observer lane must be your one non-adaptive, non-Hebbian structure: a frozen counter inside a living runtime. Book why a healing system keeps one organ that cannot heal.
- **SHIPWRIGHT**: minimal verbs, minimal joints — is the observer a second clock you refuse to build? Or the one case where duplicating a function is the *joinery-honest* answer (two independent gauges on one plank, like a scribe line and a try square)?
- **PROCESSION**: your docs teach the operator to trust the runtime. The observer is the pedagogical inversion: **the runtime must teach distrust of itself** — show the student both tallies, every tick, and make reading the gap a drill. Fail pedagogically: a student who cannot find a seeded divergence in the observer lane hasn't finished the lesson.
- **DEADBAND**: rho*F floor is *your* derivation of the audit budget. The observer asks: audited by whom, derived by which route? A self-scheduled audit is a witness for the defense. The stir demands the schedule itself be cross-derived: audit budget computed twice, from load (your way) and from event flux (the observer's way), with the **larger** of the two binding.

## Falsifiable ask

Run a **seeded-divergence test**: a hidden harness fault injects a one-unit discrepancy into the core's tally lane only (not the event stream, not the observer lane). Booked pass requires: (a) the observer lane's total provably derived without reading the core's counters (structural check, not a promise); (b) the divergence produces a **hold with both numbers in the booked reason** within the reconciliation window — no silent correction, no picking a winner; (c) subsequent transfers blocked until re-derivation reconciles or the permanent both-counts record is booked; (d) the observer lane demonstrably caused zero effects of its own. Falsifiers, any one kills: the observer sharing any accumulator with the core; divergence resolved by trusting either side automatically; the hold reason citing a single number; the observer lane able to book, refuse, or consent to anything.

## Scout's provocation

> "Every team in this tournament has built a beautiful witness for the prosecution — itself. The observer program's whole lesson, learned through forty years of fishery enforcement, is that **the count you derive from your own custody is the count with a motive**. A champion that is the sole author of its own arithmetic has not survived adversarial duty; it has merely never been cross-examined. Two books, one history, and the gap held open for all to see — that is the stir."
