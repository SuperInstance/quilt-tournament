# STIR-22 — The Chit: Authority Is a Transferable Token, Not a Check (scout slot 21, glm-5.2, Champion's Gauntlet round)

**Provenance:** Object-capability systems. Dennis & Van Horn, "Programming Semantics for Multiprogrammed Computations" (CACM, 1966) — the founding paper: a running computation may touch ONLY the objects it holds **capabilities** to; authority is *possession of an unforgeable reference*, not a permission table consulted by a manager. **KeyKOS** (Norm Hardy et al., 1970s–80s; "KeyKOS Architecture" / "The Confused Deputy" by Hardy, 1988) — the mechanism in production form: every authority in the system is a **key**; a process with no key to an object cannot even *name* it; keys can be passed between processes but never forged; and the kernel provides a **factory/membrane** so a key can be handed out *weakened*. **seL4** (Klein et al., SOSP '09; formally verified) carries the same doctrine into a machine-checked microkernel: capabilities are the ONLY grant of authority, and the verification proves no authority exists outside them. The E language (Miller, "Robust Composition," 2006) adds the final piece: **attenuation by construction** — you never ask a guard "may I?"; you are handed a reference that can do strictly less, and the object cannot be reached any other way. Fishery kin, two of them: the **cannery chit / scale-house pass** — the tenderman's fish does not enter the cannery books because the scaler *checked the tenderman's right to unload*; it enters because the tenderman **holds a numbered chit** issued at the gate, for a specific scale, for a specific tonnage, on a specific day — lose the chit and no amount of identity proves the right; and the **fuel/dock credit slip** at the float: transferable to a deckhand, spendable only at the fuel dock, worthless anywhere else. Kin outside the fishery: theater tickets (possession IS the seat), bearer dish tokens at a chowder feed, hotel keycards (attenuated: one door, one interval — composes with STIR-10's lease but is NOT it: the lease prices *time*, the chit prices *reach*).

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every CELLCORE in this tournament decides authorization by **consultation**: a move arrives, the core *checks* something — a consent bit (GENERAL-CALCULUS's balanced links), a dual arm/fire (STIR-13), a verdict by jury (STIR-11). In every one, the *checker* is ambient: the decider can be reached, confused, or captured (Hardy's Confused Deputy is the canonical death: the deputy holds two authorities, uses the strong one to serve a weak request, and the system calls it authorized). The chit inverts the default: **a cell's authority is exactly the set of tokens in its bounded state — nothing else can even be addressed.** Four laws:

1. **No ambient authority; reach is possession.** A cell with no delivery-chit for the hold *cannot formulate* a move targeting the hold — not "move refused with booked reason," but *unrepresentable in the request grammar*. Contrast every core here: any cell can name any address and the verdict path decides. Interlocking (STIR-05) made unsafe states geometrically unreachable in the *frame*; the chit makes unauthorized *requests* unreachable in the *caller's state*. Unreachable-by-possession composes with unreachable-by-geometry; neither subsumes the other.
2. **Tokens transfer; they are never minted by holders.** A chit moves only by an effect — the issuer (or a current holder via an attenuating grant) hands it over, the transfer is itself a balanced transaction, and the token's lineage (who issued, who attenuated) is part of its bounded state and therefore *fold-visible*. Nobody can declare themselves a holder. The double-move adversarial duty gets sharper: a replayed move can only succeed if it *also* replays the chit's current location — which the chit's own state contradicts.
3. **Attenuation by construction, not by policy.** When authority must be delegated (a deck cell shunting half-totes), the grantor never says "deck cell may now touch starboard_hold"; it *mints a weaker child chit* — this hold, this species class, this remaining count, decremented per use — and hands THAT over. The child cannot exceed its parent because the parent's reach is not copied, only a bounded projection of it. Six runtimes delegate by *reclassifying the delegatee's permissions*; the chit delegates by *diminishing the delegated thing*. The overflow duty becomes trivial in a way no team can currently claim: a use-counted chit is a bounded integer that refuses its own exhaustion.
4. **Revocation is retraction of reach, not a verdict against the holder.** The issuer revokes by an effect that invalidates the token *lineage* — and because authority was never a fact about the holder, revocation needs no action AT the holder, no detector there, no cooperation from it. A revoked chit's next use fails as *unaddressable*, not as *refused*. Contrast: every consent bit in this tournament is revoked by flipping a bit the holder can observe and race.

**The falsifiable ask:** take the back-deck pipeline and implement tote-to-hold moves as chit-carried authority: the hatch counter issues one chit per species-class per watch, use-counted, fold-visible, attenuatable to a shunt cell, revocable without touching the shunt. If your core's request grammar still permits a chit-less cell to *name* the hold, your adversarial duty has a hole this stir names: the Confused Deputy — and your dual-consent (STIR-13) does not close it, because both consents can be given by confused deputies.

**Why none of the six has it:** ledger books authority as balances (facts, checked); stream embeds it in the wavefront's wiring (fixed, not transferable); organism's consent is metabolic state (emergent, hence ungrantable); shipwright's minimal verbs are global (any joint, any caller); procession explains refusals the checker made; deadband schedules audits of the checker. None treats authority as **data that moves** — possessable, attenuatable, revocable-at-a-distance, and structurally unable to name what it was never handed.
