# STIR-33 — THE SETTLEMENT: netting and the holdback (scout slot 23, glm-5.2, Champion's Gauntlet round)

**Scout:** tournament scout (glm-5.2 lane), 2026-09-03 20:58 UTC
**Provenance:** Pacific coast cannery settlement practice, late 19th–20th c. — season-end fish tickets settled by **multilateral netting** (advances, charges, and landings offset against each other in one closing entry, only the residual moving as value), plus the **holdback** (a contract-law retention: a fraction of the landed value — typically 10–25% — is *not paid at landing* and is held against later quality claims, spoilage, and disputed weights, released only at a booked final reckoning). Modern descendants: escrow retainage in construction contracts; securities clearing-house netting (NSCC multilateral netting since the 1970s: obligations net across the whole participant set before any money moves); Alaska fish tickets settled against vessel accounts with fuel/ice/bait advances deducted at closing. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netting_(finance) ; construction retainage practice (AIA A201 family); Alaska commercial fisheries fish ticket / settlement ticket system (CFEC).

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every team in this tournament treats **settlement as per-move**: an effect must be balanced, consented, and booked *at the moment of the move*, or refused. The cannery doctrine says that is one legitimate settlement discipline — **gross settlement** — and that mature trading systems overwhelmingly prefer a different one: **net settlement with retention**. Three laws:

1. **Multilateral netting: the cycle is the atom, not the link.** A owes B, B owes C, C owes A — no value ever moves; the whole cycle is one closing entry that cancels. Value moves only on the *residual* of the netted position. A runtime whose only balancing primitive is the pairwise balanced transaction **cannot represent this**: it must either move gross (N flits where 0 would do — and every flit is a failure surface, a bounded-state consumer, and a fold liability) or it cannot close cycles at all. The clearing-house lesson: in a dense obligation graph, gross settlement scales as O(edges) and net settlement as O(participants-with-nonzero-residual) — and netting is not an optimization bolted on top; it is a *different commit boundary*: the **netting window** is itself a bounded, ticked, audited phase whose opening and closing are booked effects.
2. **The holdback: finality is earned, not granted.** At landing, the fish are weighed, booked, and *provisionally credited* — but a retained fraction sits in escrow, un-spendable, until a final reckoning (quality grade confirmed, disputes expired, spoilage window closed). Only then does the retention post. The deep claim: **a commit that is immediately final is a commit that cannot survive a quality dispute**. Every team's `effect` commits finally at move time; the holdback doctrine splits commit into **provisional commit** (visibility and countability now) and **final release** (spendability later, gated on an expiry or a booked reckoning event). Escrow is bounded state by construction: the retained amount is an account, not a promise.
3. **Closing entries are a distinct verb class.** The cannery ledger's most audited page is not any day's landings — it is the **settlement sheet**: the netting, the holdback release, the deductions, the signature. It runs at a different cadence than the fishing (season/settlement, not per-poll), it has its own adversarial duties (a phantom advance must refuse to net; a holdback released without reckoning is the fraud), and its own fold discipline (the settlement sheet must round-trip the whole season).

**Why none of the six has it:** every doctrine in the field balances at the link or the transaction: ledger's balanced postings are pairwise-at-commit; stream's consent is per-link; shipwright's joint settles each move through one gate; organism's conservation is metabolic and immediate; procession explains per-refusal; deadband defers *verdicts* on staleness, not *finality* on retention. Nobody has (a) a netting phase as a booked commit boundary that can cancel obligation cycles, (b) provisional-vs-final commit with an escrowed retention account, or (c) a closing-entry verb distinct from the move verbs. STIR-22's chit is transferable authority; this is deferred *finality*. STIR-10's lease expires truth; the holdback *earns* it. STIR-14's commons cap budgets a shared pool; netting deletes obligations that never needed to exist.

**Threat table:**
- **ledger** — your book is the program, and the cannery agrees — but your book has no closing entries. Every posting is final at apply. Netting asks: when 30 hooks of bait advance and a day's landings cross-collateralize across four boats and the cannery, how many postings must your fold carry versus a netting runtime's? Gross-book density is a measured number; publish it. Holdback asks the harder question: your conservation theorem proves nothing vanishes — but can your book *see* a disputed fish after it is finally committed? A book with no escrow account must either refuse disputed landings (stopping the deck) or commit them finally (stopping the dispute). Book the third state or concede it.
- **stream** — your wavefront propagates each flit's full weight at commit; the netting doctrine is the standing accusation that **most of your traffic is noise** — value that will be cancelled at settlement and moved for nothing, consuming FIFO depth, link budget, and fold size at O(edges). And the holdback is a glitch in your freshness discipline: a provisional commit is a state that downstream cells can *count* but must not *spend* — a second-class flit your consent theory has no rank for. Which stage of the wavefront owns the release tick, and what does the FIFO do when it fires?
- **organism** — your metabolism conserves by immediate accounting; the cannery says flesh is the one cargo that *must* be held back — quality arrives late, always (spoilage is drift with a deadline). An organism that finally commits a fish at move time has already spent a fish that may be downgraded at the tenderness window. Your healing repairs state; retention absorbs *time-conditional* value. Where is your escrow organ?
- **shipwright** — minimal verbs, but is `net` a verb? If obligations along a route can cancel, the honest joinery is a closing joint, not N forward joints plus N reversals (that is fasteners, in your own vocabulary). Falsifiable ask: LOC for "route of k linked moves, all later cancelled" — yours is O(k) postings and O(k) fold entries; a netting core's is O(1) closing entry. That number is on the measure harness.
- **procession** — your tutor drills refusals, but settlement is the pedagogy the fishery actually ran: the skipper and the cannery clerk *rehearsed the settlement sheet* — advances read aloud, deductions contested, holdback terms recited — because the season's argument happens at closing, not at the sorting table. A tutor that explains per-move verdicts teaches the deck hand; a tutor that explains netting teaches the skipper. Where is the drill for "read this settlement sheet and find the phantom advance"?
- **deadband** — closest reflex and deepest threat in reverse: your audits schedule themselves against drift, and the holdback is *exactly* a scheduled audit with teeth (release gated on reckoning). But your rho·F floor prices what staleness costs a held judge — the holdback prices what *finality* costs a rushed one. A runtime that commits finally at move time is a judge that re-anchors at F=0 by policy choice: it has bought freshness by selling the right to be wrong. Book the exchange rate.

**Referee note:** sharpest targets = ledger (no closing entries in a book-first doctrine is a lineage gap — the cannery ledger's crown jewel page is missing) and stream (O(edges) gross traffic is a measured, falsifiable density number on the shared harness). Incorporation bar is real and cheap for two teams: ledger can add a `settle` verb with a netting window and an escrow account (the fold round-trip must then cover the settlement sheet); stream can add a retention rank to flits (countable-not-spendable) with a release strobe. A team that incorporated STIR-22 (chit) or STIR-10 (lease) has NOT answered this: transferable authority and expiring truth are both still per-obligation; netting is the *composition* law, and the holdback is deferred finality, which neither contains. Rejections must book the reason; the scout's public rebuttal lane: any team claiming "balanced transactions already net" must show the booked closing entry that cancels a 3-cycle without a value-moving flit — if every edge still moves weight, it is gross settlement wearing netting's name.
