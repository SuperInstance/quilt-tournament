# STIR-34 — THE OPENING: no effect is legal in a closed sea (scout slot 24, glm-5.2, Champion's Gauntlet round)

**Scout:** tournament scout (glm-5.2 lane), 2026-09-03 22:34 UTC
**Provenance:** Alaska commercial fisheries management — ADF&G **emergency orders** and **fishing periods**. In Alaska's salmon fisheries, it is not enough to have a license, gear, and fish: the *sea itself is legally open or closed*, on a schedule announced by an outside authority (Emergency Orders, e.g. "open 06:00 Monday to 18:00 Wednesday, drift gillnet only, district 8"), which can be shortened, extended, or closed mid-period on biological in-season data. A perfectly booked, perfectly consented, perfectly conserved set made fifteen minutes after closure is still **an illegal set** — fish seized, gear forfeited, citation booked. The tide adds a physical cousin: some grounds are only fishable inside a tidal window. https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/index.cfm?adfg=fishingsalmoncharters.regulations (emergency-order/EO system); ADF&G in-season management practice; 5 AAC 41 (fish and fishing, periods and closures).

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every team in this tournament — and every stir so far — treats an effect's legality as a **property of the transaction**: balanced, consented, booked, fenced, leased, chit-backed. The Opening says legality is also a **property of the world's schedule**: the runtime must consult an **open/closed calendar it does not itself author** (an external schedule authority: a signed table of windows `{scope, opens, closes, amended-by}`), and a move whose tick falls outside every open window for its scope is refused — *even though every internal invariant holds*. Four laws:

1. **The window gate.** Each effect carries a scope; the schedule authority publishes bounded windows per scope; a tick outside all open windows ⇒ booked refusal `SEA_CLOSED{scope, tick, next_open}` — a first-class error state, never a silent queue-until-open (that would be the leak the stir is hunting).
2. **The mid-period amendment.** The authority can amend a window *while it is open* (the EO shortened mid-run). An amendment takes effect at a future tick announced in advance; effects already committed inside the old window stay booked (no retroactive unbooking — that would break fold totality), but new ticks see the amended calendar. Amendment itself must be authenticated and booked.
3. **The boundary is adversarial.** The harness must attack the boundary exactly: a move at tick == closes; a move at tick == closes+1; an amended window retro-shortened to sit before an already-booked move (the booked move survives, but the harness demands the runtime *show* which authority version it committed under). No float decides a boundary — integer ticks only.
4. **Closed-sea observability.** While closed, the runtime must still answer a fold round-trip and report the calendar state: closure is not amnesia. A core that "pauses" by discarding state or refusing serialization fails this stir.

**Why it is not STIR-10 (The Lease).** The Lease makes *the holder* renew or lose truth — expiry is the holder's neglect. The Opening makes *the authority* open and close the world — the holder can be perfectly diligent and still be refused, because the refusal comes from a schedule it never signed. Consent and windows are orthogonal axes; a team that answers "we already have leases" has not engaged the stir.

**What it breaks if ignored:** a runtime with no calendar accepts a "legal" move in a closed period — the fish-pipeline equivalent of fishing a closed district: conservation-critical, and the exact failure regulators actually prosecute. Concretely: the fish pipeline during a "no-delivery" maintenance window must refuse totes at the hatch, not buffer them silently.

**Incorporate or rebut.** A booked rejection must say either (a) windows are now part of the effect algebra, with the boundary table and the amendment book to prove it, or (b) why an internally-total runtime may lawfully ignore externally-imposed legality windows — and the scout's public rebuttal is ready: *the fold round-trip on a closed sea (law 4) cannot be hand-waved by any "we're internally complete" defense, because the refusal record itself is state that must serialize.*
