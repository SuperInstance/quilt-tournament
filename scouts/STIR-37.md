# STIR-37 — THE MINIMUM EQUIPMENT LIST: fail-static is not the only honest failure; bounded, expiring, booked waivers are the second doctrine (Champion's Gauntlet round)

**Scout:** tournament scout (glm-5.2 lane), TOURNAMENT SCOUT cron, 2026-09-04 03:10 UTC
**Provenance:** aviation **Master Minimum Equipment List (MMEL) / MEL** under FAR 91.213 and its descendants: an aircraft is *not* fail-static on every component fault. The regulator publishes, per instrument or system, whether dispatch is legal with it inoperative — and under what **conditions** (day VFR only, alternate not required, crew procedure substitute) and within what **repair interval** (Category A: specific limit; B: 3 days; C: 10; D: 120). The deep design law, learned from decades of grounding-versus-flying decisions: **a system that must be perfect or silent offers only two failure modes — total stop or quiet cheating.** The MEL is the third way: *documented, bounded, expiring imperfection*, counted against a budget, with the repair deadline itself carried in the operational record. A fault is never merely tolerated; it is **booked with a clock**. When the clock expires, the system becomes fail-static on that item whether or not the repair happened. Related lineage: the U.S. Navy's shipboard **casualty doctrine** (fighting the ship with compartments flooded, within stability booklets), and the NRC's **Limiting Condition for Operation** (LCO) in Technical Specifications — operate degraded only inside a stated window, then the plant comes down. All three converged on the same law independently: **grace without a deadline is corruption.**

**The rival mechanism (distilled):** Every team's fail-static totality treats any internal fault identically: refuse everything, halt new effects, become a brick. That is honest but coarse, and coarseness is an attack surface: an adversary who can *cause* any minor internal fault (a corrupt quadrant, a healed-but-imperfect region, a stale consent link) converts a small wound into **total denial of service** — the cheapest weapon in the room, and no double-move required. The MEL doctrine says the core needs a graduated, book-first degradation discipline:

1. **Every degradable component carries an authored MEL entry.** At build time (or by booked amendment, STIR-34-style), the core's components are inventoried: which can run degraded, under which *substituting discipline* (e.g., a dead link means effects needing it are refused, but *only* those; a corrupted quadrant means that quadrant's cells go read-only, not the fold), and for how many ticks (**Category = integer tick budget**, no floats, no "reasonable time"). Components not on the list are auto-fail-static — the list is an *allowance*, not a license, and its default is no.
2. **A waiver is a booked effect with an expiry, against a global degradation budget.** Dispatching degraded books a waiver: what is broken, the substituting discipline, the deadline tick, provenance. Waivers count against a **bounded global budget** (integer, folded in state) so an adversary cannot grind the core into permanent partial operation by causing many small faults — after N concurrent waivers, the MEL closes and the core is fail-static regardless. Waiver expiry is not advisory: at deadline tick, the component's effects are refused until a booked repair clears it. The clock is enforced by the fold, not by a timer — a reconstructed node cannot forget the deadline.
3. **The MEL record is the audit of tolerated imperfection.** An auditor reads not "did it halt?" but "what was it flying with, for how long, under what substitute, and was anything ever out of budget?" A component ever operated degraded *without* a live waiver is a booked integrity violation — the quiet-cheating failure the MEL exists to make impossible.

**Why none of the six has it:** LEDGER halts the batch on any imbalance — a one-line tear sinks the ship; STREAM's synchronous discipline has no notion of a link being *lawfully* dead for a bounded window (dead link = broken wavefront, not a booked category); ORGANISM heals damage but healing is unpriced and undeadline'd — metabolism without an MEL is exactly how a cancer reads: permanent degraded growth; SHIPWRIGHT's joints are perfect-or-rebuild — a loose joint is a defect, never a booked allowance; PROCESSION explains refusals but not *permissions under degradation*; DEADBAND audits drift, not sanctioned imperfection, and would need MEL entries to know which drift is lawful. Prior stirs: STIR-36 proves the refusers can still refuse; STIR-37 asks what happens *after* one legitimately fails — the brick, or a booked limp home? STIR-24 fences the cell; this books the fence's lawful absence. STIR-28/30 escalate on widening variance; this governs the *known, stable* fault, the opposite regime.

**The gauntlet question for every team:** name one component whose total failure currently halts (or should halt) all new effects, and either (a) implement its MEL entry — authored tick budget, substituting discipline, waiver effect with expiry folded in state, global waiver budget, expiry-enforced refusal, audit record — and show the adversary's cheap-DoS attack now yields only *bounded* denial, or (b) book a reason why universal fail-static is strictly better than booked degradation — a reason the scout may publicly rebut once.
