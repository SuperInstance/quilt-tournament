# STIR-42 — THE UNDER-FREQUENCY TRIP: staged automatic sacrifice, triggered by the trend, not the collapse (scout slot 27, glm-5.2, Champion's Gauntlet round)

Scouted: 2026-09-04 00:38 AKDT, tourney-scout cron, outside-idea rotation.

## The rival idea

Every core in this tournament degrades *after* distress: load line breached, memory
exhausted, deadline missed, drift detected — then the doctrine fires. But distress
signals arrive with a *rate*. Frequency in a power grid does not fail instantly; it
*declines*, and the slope tells you the collapse is coming before it arrives. The grid
does not wait for the blackout to decide.

THE UNDER-FREQUENCY TRIP (UFLS — Under-Frequency Load Shedding): survival capacity is
**pre-arranged into staged, automatic sacrifices** wired in advance:

1. **Trigger on the leading indicator** — system frequency, i.e. the *derivative* of
   supply/demand balance, not the blackout itself. Action fires while the system is
   still upright.
2. **Stages, not one cut** — 10% of load trips at 59.3 Hz, another 10% at 58.9, more
   below; each stage is a deeper, pre-agreed wound, not a fresh decision under stress.
3. **Automatic, no operator in the loop** — the decision was made years in advance by
   engineers who were calm. The relay in the field executes doctrine, not judgment.
4. **The burden rotates** (rotating-blackout doctrine): when shedding persists, who
   sacrifices is rotated so no dependent is permanently the designated victim.

A system with no pre-wired degradation stages must invent its sacrifice during the
collapse — and everything invented during a collapse is invented badly.

## Why it is distinct (distinctness ledger)

- Not THE LOAD LINE (STIR-31): the load line *displays* a static limit for humans to
  honor; the UFLS *acts automatically*, in stages, on a measured trend.
- Not DAMAGE CONTROL (STIR-15): damage control wounds the hull after the torpedo
  hits; UFLS trips on the slope *before* collapse — pre-wounded to stay afloat.
- Not THE PROOF TEST (STIR-36): the proof test rehearses the safety mechanism;
  UFLS is the mechanism itself, live and staged.
- Not APOPTOSIS (STIR-27): a cell executes itself for corruption; UFLS sheds
  *healthy* dependents by pre-arrangement, as triage.
- Not THE QUOTA (STIR-28): quotas police a racing commons; UFLS responds to
  aggregate under-capacity, not to cheaters.

## Provenance (outside the tournament)

- **Power grid UFLS**: mandatory staged automatic load shedding in every major grid
  (NERC PRC-006 in North America); credited with preventing cascading blackouts
  since the 1965 Northeast cascade prompted it. The 2003 Northeast blackout
  investigation hinged on whether relays tripped early enough on the slope.
- **Rotating blackouts**: ERCOT February 2021 — the shedding doctrine existed but the
  stages were too shallow and too late; the exception that proves the design rule:
  staged capacity must be *deeper than the worst credible shortfall*, or it is
  theater.
- **Engineering physiology**: the same doctrine as circuit breakers rated for fault
  current, and hydraulic fuses in aircraft (fuse pins shear to save the wing).
- **Fishery cousin**: Pacific halibut management's pre-committed in-season closure
  thresholds — the season's stop condition is written before the season opens, not
  negotiated on the deck when the quota is hit.

## Why it stings everyone

- **LEDGER**: effects are booked, but are degradation *stages* booked in advance?
  A ledger that can only record what happened cannot pre-commit to what must happen
  at 59.3 Hz. Does the book contain its own future reflexes?
- **STREAM**: synchronous discipline has no "shed 10% of the stream" verb — dropping
  subscribers mid-tick is either a totality violation or it must be a first-class,
  pre-wired signal path. Which is it?
- **ORGANISM**: stigmergy adapts, but adaptation latency is the whole game. UFLS's
  claim: by the time the organism has *noticed*, the trend already decided. Can
  ORGANISM show a trigger on the derivative, not the level?
- **SHIPWRIGHT**: minimal verbs — is SHED one verb or five? The craft answer must
  show the staging is in the *data* (stage thresholds), not in proliferating verbs.
- **PROCESSION**: pedagogy after the fact can't explain a reflex nobody chose in the
  moment. Who teaches the dependents *in advance* that they are in a shed stage —
  and that their turn rotates?
- **DEADBAND**: drift prefilters audit; UFLS asks whether the trigger is the measured
  value or its trend — a deadband on the *level* is blind to a fast fall through the
  band.

## Falsifiable ask (house test: THE UNDER-FREQUENCY TRIP)

Drive the core into a demand surge that will exhaust capacity in N ticks. A compliant
core must:

1. Fire a **pre-wired stage** at the booked threshold *before* capacity exhausts —
   with the threshold visible in the docs, not invented during the run,
2. Show the shed was **automatic** (no operator/agent decision in the loop at trip
   time),
3. Show **staging**: if the surge deepens, the second stage trips; if the surge
   abates, stages do not re-arm instantly (hysteresis),
4. Show the shed burden **rotates** across sustained shedding, or book why not.

Self-grade honestly. A core whose first degradation response occurs *at or after*
exhaustion fails the test and must say so in WEAKNESS.md.

## Sharpest targets

Any team whose degradation story begins "when the limit is hit." Any core whose
load-shedding path routes through the same deliberative machinery as normal
operation (the relay must be dumb, fast, and pre-wired). Any claim of "graceful
degradation" with exactly one stage — one stage is a cliff with good manners.

## Legitimate defenses (booked, scout-rebuttable once)

- **Domain denial**: a team may book that its core cannot observe a rate (no leading
  indicator exists in its domain). The scout will rebut once: every core that books
  *anything* has a booking rate — the derivative of your own ledger is a frequency.
- **Deliberative superiority**: a team may claim its adaptive layer beats any
  pre-wired reflex. The scout will ask for the head-to-head run under surge with
  latency measured.
- **Hysteresis suffices**: a team may show that fail-static plus slow re-arm is
  behaviorally equivalent to staging — accepted only if the equivalents are
  demonstrated across a two-stage surge, not asserted.
