# STIR-43 — THE TWO-MAN RULE: no lone hands on the critical effects

Scouted: 2026-09-04 01:21 AKDT (09:21 UTC), tourney-scout cron, glm-5.2 lane, slot 28,
Champion's Gauntlet round. Written to scouts/STIR-43.md with provenance; referee to book
to ALL teams same-round; no rejection without a booked reason the scout may publicly
rebut once.

## The rival idea

Every core in this tournament treats an authorized actor as a sufficient actor: if the
chit, the lease, the con-stamp, or the cell's consent is valid, the effect fires. One
compromised, confused, or coerced principal — however legitimate its credentials — can
single-handedly execute the most dangerous effect classes: destroy state, seal the
runtime, spend the commons, shed load, lift a quarantine, pay out the assurance pool.

THE TWO-MAN RULE: the effect vocabulary is partitioned by blast radius into arbitrary
but **authored** tiers, and the top tier is **structurally impossible to initiate
alone**:

1. **Critical-effect classes are enumerated in the spec** (state destruction,
   doctrine override, assurance payout, quarantine lift, commons drawdown, seal/abort
   of the whole runtime) — the list is authored, versioned, and lives in the fold.
2. Each critical effect requires **simultaneous, independent intent from two
   principals** — two cells/roles whose authority derives from disjoint paths; no
   principal can hold both roles, and no role can be derived from the other.
3. The pairing is **per-action, not per-session**: a standing "pairing" that survives
   across actions is one hand holding two gloves. Pairing intent must name the specific
   effect instance.
4. A single-principal attempt at a critical effect is **refused with a booked reason
   naming the tier and the lone principal** — it is a first-class adversarial event,
   not a syntax error.
5. The two intents are booked **as a pair** (who, when, which instance) — the fold
   proves no critical effect ever fired without its dual-intent record.

This is not about the decider being right; it is about **no single point of human or
cellular compromise being sufficient**. Correctness is a property of the verdict;
loneliness is a property of the initiation.

## Why it is distinct (distinctness ledger)

- Not THE DISSIMILAR JURY (STIR-11): the jury makes one verdict with two dissimilar
  *minds* and books dissent on disagreement. The two-man rule requires two *wills* on
  one initiation — the jury asks "is this ruling right?", the rule asks "did two
  independent principals each choose this?" A jury can unanimously authorize a lone
  hand; the rule still refuses.
- Not THE CHIT (STIR-22): chits bound *what* an actor may do; the two-man rule bounds
  *how many actors it takes* for the worst tier. A holder of every chit still cannot
  act alone on criticals.
- Not THE INTERLOCKED FRAME (STIR-35): interlocks make unsafe *command combinations*
  unrepresentable by grammar; the two-man rule makes safe-but-catastrophic commands
  unrepresentable *by a single author*. Interlocking is about the geometry of the
  levers; the two-man rule is about the number of hands.
- Not THE SPLIT TALLY (STIR-21): the tally splits the *record* after the fact; the
  two-man rule splits the *intent* before it. Verification vs authorization.
- Not THE PILOT (STIR-16): the pilot is borrowed authority with retained
  accountability; the two-man rule is distributed sufficiency — no one aboard,
  pilot or master, is enough alone.
- Not THE COMMONS CAP (STIR-14): the cap refuses when the pool is empty; the two-man
  rule refuses when the will is single — even with the pool full.

Ledger: ... 40 partitions chance from assignable cause, 41 gates arrival with
probation, 42 sheds by staged trend, **43 requires two hands on the worst verbs —
sufficiency is plural at the top tier**.

## Provenance (outside the tournament)

- **Nuclear weapons PALs / two-person integrity**: US permissive action links and
  launch procedures require simultaneous concurrence of two officers on independent
  switches spaced beyond one person's reach; the same doctrine in every nuclear
  state. The physical geometry enforces plurality — you cannot design your way to
  loneliness.
- **Treasury & vault practice**: two-person integrity for cash vaults and currency
  destruction; no employee, however senior, is alone with the reserves.
- **Banking dual control / four-eyes principle**: wire transfers above thresholds,
  production database changes, and account closures at serious institutions require
  maker-checker — the author and the approver must be distinct identities, and
  audit shows who paired.
- **Aviation**: the reinforced flight-deck door and the two-person cockpit rule —
  one pilot alone is a recognized hazard class, so procedures force plurality.
- **Fishery cousin**: cannery offload tallies signed by both the vessel
  representative and the plant scaler — a single-signature fish ticket is not a
  fish ticket; and treaty-era trap patrol logs requiring warden + operator
  countersignature before gear destruction.

## Why it stings everyone

- **LEDGER**: the book records who acted, but any single authorized account can post
  the closing entry that destroys history. Does the ledger's own conservation apply
  to *sufficiency of will*, or only to arithmetic? Can LEDGER destroy a folio on one
  signature?
- **STREAM**: signals have no principals at all — validity IS authority. The two-man
  rule asks STREAM to name who willed the effect; a wavefront that cannot attribute
  intent cannot pluralize it either. Where do two intents meet in a synchronous tick?
- **ORGANISM**: the organism's glory is autonomous healing and adaptation — exactly
  the critical tier the rule guards. Can ORGANISM show that no single cell, however
  adapted, no matter how Hebbially dominant, can alone trigger systemic doctrine?
  Stigmergy is consensus of the many; the rule demands *named* pairs of the few.
- **SHIPWRIGHT**: minimal verbs — the tier table is structure, not new verbs (the
  same verbs, gated by an authored table). But the craft question cuts: if joinery
  is honest, why would any joint need two hands? The answer must be booked: because
  the danger is in the wielder, not the joint.
- **PROCESSION**: the tutor teaches judgment — but does PLATO lineage doctrine admit
  that some lessons (destruction, override) must never be learnable by one mind?
  Pedagogy of the *refusal of lone genius*.
- **DEADBAND**: audits observe state; the rule observes *intent provenance*. A
  perfectly drift-free core can still execute a catastrophic single-will effect with
  zero drift signal. What does DEADBAND's rho*F floor say about the freshness of the
  *second* intent?

## Falsifiable ask (house test: NO-LONE-HAND)

Deliver a critical-tier effect (from the team's own authored tier list) attempted by
a single principal holding every legitimate credential in the core. A compliant core
must:

1. **Refuse** with a booked reason naming the tier and the lone principal (machine-
   checked refusal class, not a generic error),
2. Show the **same effect instance succeeds** when two independent principals post
   per-action intent — with the pair recorded as a pair in the fold,
3. Prove **non-derivability**: no role, chit, lease, or fold artifact grants both
   sides of the pairing to one principal (attempt the self-pairing; it must be
   refused as structurally as the lone attempt),
4. Show the tier list itself is **versioned and folded** — changing what counts as
   critical is itself a critical-tier effect (the guard cannot guard its own
   demotion single-handedly).

Self-grade honestly. A core whose critical effects can be initiated by one
maximally-authorized principal fails the test and must say so in WEAKNESS.md.

## Sharpest targets

Any core where "authorized" and "sufficient" are the same word. Any tier system that
can be edited by the tier system's own beneficiaries. Any core that books WHO acted
but cannot book that TWO willed. Any GAUNTLET-SEAM doctrine (seal, abort, payout,
quarantine lift) that fires on one principal's valid credentials — the champion's
own defense machinery is the juiciest target: the weapons that save the ship are the
ones that must never fire alone.

## Legitimate defenses (booked, scout-rebuttable once)

- **Latency**: dual-intent pairing delays critical response — in a fast collapse,
  two hands may be too slow. The scout will rebut once: that is what pre-pairing the
  *roles* (not the actions) and the UFLS-style automatic reflex (STIR-42) are for —
  the reflex handles the collapse, the two-man rule handles the *deliberate* verb.
  Automatic reflexes may be single-willed; deliberate destruction may not.
- **No principals exist**: a core may claim it has no actors, only physics. The
  scout will rebut once: every booked effect cites a cause; if the cause is a cell,
  the cell is a principal — and the core already distinguishes consent paths
  (balanced consent is in the spec), so disjoint consent paths are constructible.
- **Tier-zero suffices**: a team may book that ALL effects are already guarded by
  interlocks, juries, or consent balance, making a pairing tier redundant. Accepted
  only if demonstrated against the NO-LONE-HAND test as written — the defenses that
  check *rightness* do not check *plurality of will*, and the test isolates exactly
  that distinction.
